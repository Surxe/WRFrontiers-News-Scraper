PILOT TWO NEW ROBOTS

Two new formidable friends are joining your Hangar with the Spring Update. Meet Orochi and Purifier. 

Orochi

Orochi is a scouting Robot built for speed and reconnaissance. Orochi highlights enemies, pings them, and prepares for a team fight with its allies. Its ability, Spotlight, enables a speed boost and allows allied weapons to lock on to highlighted enemies.

Purifier 

Purifier is the best bodyguard for your team. A heavy, slow-moving Robot, Purifier keeps its allies close, buffing them as they head into battle. Its ability, Zealot, buffs surrounding allies, enabling them to deal increased damage. This damage bonus increased with every enemy shield penetrated and Robot destroyed. 

BATTLE ON A NEW MAP: CATALYST

This volcanic Map isn’t for the faint of heart. Available across all three game modes, Catalyst is an explosive experience where lava can take you out just as fast as a foe. 

UPGRADE YOUR ARSENAL

Two new Weapons and a brand-new Ability are yours to wield in the Spring Update. Slow enemies and inflict damage with the new Minefield Ability, sure to shock unsuspecting enemies.

Burn up the competition with the new flamethrower Incinerator, and cause major AoE damage with the Rampant light grenade launcher. 

EXPERIMENT AT THE FIRING RANGE

Test out your Robot and Titan builds on the new Firing Range! Battle against static Robots and aggressive AI to make sure you’re operating at the highest levels of power possible. 

POWER UP YOUR TITANS

Titans are the most powerful tool you have on the battlefield, and now you can upgrade and customize your fearsome friends in the new Titan Constructor. Outfit your newly leveled Titans with brand-new Weapons. 

  • Bayonet (Alpha): This electromagnetic medium-range pulse laser packs a shocking punch. 
  • Jigsaw (Grim): Fire a blade projectile that can ricochet from surfaces and tear into enemy targets.
  • Scrubber (Matriarch): A pair of multi-barrel machine guns will wipe out the competition.
