Claiming Rewards From Twitch Drops

Your rewards will appear in your Drops Inventory when you’ve accumulated enough watch time to earn them. Make sure your account is linked, and be sure to claim them while Drops are active. 

How To Link Your Twitch And War Robots: Frontiers Accounts

To claim your items, you must link your War Robots: Frontiers account with your Twitch account:

  • Log in via the official website.
    • Make sure you log in with the platform and account you want to receive your Twitch Drops on. For example, if you play War Robots: Frontiers on Steam you should log in using Steam authentication. 
  • Select ‘Link with Twitch’ and confirm the connection.
  • You’re all set! Watch Drop-enabled War Robots: Frontiers streams on Twitch to start earning Twitch Drops.
