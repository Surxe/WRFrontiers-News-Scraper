Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers (Early Access) and available following a short period of maintenance on April 26, 2024. 

  • Improved client performance in Battle by optimizing parts of the interface and some visual effects.
  • Fixed an issue that caused audio static to play when killing enemies using a Legendary Pilot.
  • Improved the brightness setting screen, so changes made are now more noticeable.
  • Fixed an issue that prevented audio cues from activating when removing parts in the Hangar. 
  • Fixed an issue that could cause players to get stuck in matchmaking. Players will now receive an “unable to connect to the server” message if a Battle cannot be found. 
  • Fixed a bug where incorrect values of damage dealt were being shown in Daily Contracts.
  • Fixed an issue where friends appeared to be duplicated in the Social Tab.
