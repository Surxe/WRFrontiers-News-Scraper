Attention, Pilots! The following patch will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on September 9, 2025.

New Content

  • Added a new Game Mode: Team Deathmatch +
    • Team Deathmatch + is available alongside the regular Team Deathmatch Mode.
    • Score points by destroying enemy War Robots or by collecting Datacubes.
    • The first team to reach the point limit wins.
  • Added a new Map: Horizon One
  • This patch introduces the new Dei Ex Machina Event Pass.
    • The Event Pass features many rewards including the weapons ‘MLx2’ & ‘Bisector’, and the War Robots ‘Ceres’ & ‘Anansi’.
  • Added a new Animated Wrap: Drakeplate
  • Added a new Cycle Gear: Iron Veil
    • Reduces all incoming damage by 90% for 3.5s.
  • Added a new Weapon: Apollo (Light, Electromagnetic, Sniper)

Gameplay Adjustments

Balance Changes

Alpha

  • Reduced the radius for ‘Firestorm’ at max. Level from 97.5m to 85m.
  • Reduced the radius for ‘Firestorm’ at min. Level from 75m to 71m.

Matriarch

  • Increased the active duration of ‘Nanite Field’ from 12s to 15s.
  • Decreased the Armor Regeneration value of ‘Nanite Field’ from 4.7% to 3.3%.

Volta

  • Increased the max. range of ‘Tesla Coil’ from 150m to 250m.
  • Increased the max. range of ‘Short Circuit’ from 175m to 250m.
  • Increased the projectile speed of ‘Cascade’ from 52,000 to 90,000.
  • Increased the Armor Damage of ‘Cascade’ from 4,700 to 5,200.

Anansi

  • Increased the radius for ‘Camouflage Web’ from 45m to 55m.
  • Increased the active duration for ‘Camouflage Web’ from 18s to 25s.
  • Decreased the cooldown of ‘Camouflage Web’ from 35s to 30s.

Ares

  • Reduced the Shield Regeneration of Ares’ shoulders from 5,940 to 3,200.
  • Reduced the Shield cooldown of Ares’ shoulders from 7.7% to 5%.
  • Increased the Armor value of Ares’ shoulders from 51,800 to 56,600.

Ceres

  • Increased the active duration of ‘Rejuvenator’ from 8s to 10s.
  • Decreased the Armor Regeneration value of ‘‘Rejuvenator’ ’ from 2.2% to 1.5%.

Fenrir

  • Reduced the bonus damage value of ‘Unchained’ from 50% to 25%.
  • Increased the health regeneration rate provided by ‘Vampirism’ from 45% to 55%.

Fury

  • Increased the ammo refill rate of ‘Instant Reload’ from 70% to 100%.
  • Reduced the cooldown of ‘Instant Reload’ from 25s to 22s.

Dragoon

  • Increased the magazine size from 3 to 4.

Scatter

  • Increased the Armor Damage from 6,630 to 7,800.

Shocktrain

  • Increased magazine size from 3 to 4.
  • Decreased the effective range from 450m to 410m.

Ghost Turret

  • Decreased the damage from 1,100 to 850.
  • Increased the active duration from 13s to 15s.

Progression

  • The early progression of the game has been updated:
    • The visuals and mechanics of the Tutorials have been updated.
    • Tutorial steps have been adjusted.
    • New progression rewards have been added after battles.
  • Added 15 new Levels to Account Level Progression.
    • Added blueprints for the War Robot ‘Loki’ as rewards.
    • Added the blueprint for the weapon ‘Halo’ as reward.

General

  • Added conditional colors for Gears to differentiate between allied and enemy Gears.
    • Umbrella (Cycle Gear): Enemy VFX have been recolored to red.
    • Blast Wave (Supply Gear): Allied VFX have been recolored to blue.
    • Echo Barrage (Orochi): Enemy VFX have been recolored to red.
    • Energy Dome (Varangian): Enemy VFX have been recolored to red.
    • Battle Order (Lancelot): Enemy VFX have been recolored to grey.
    • Short Circuit (Volta): Enemy VFX have been recolored to red.
    • Ripper (Grim): Enemy VFX have been recolored to red, allied VFX have been recolored to yellow.
    • Firestorm (Alpha): Enemy VFX have been recolored to red, allied VFX have been recolored to blue.
  • Added SFX to level 5 Pilot Talents of common pilots.
  • Added a new icon for Animated Wraps.

Workshop

  • The Workshop has been reworked:
    • A new system for Custom and Factory Robots has been added. Players can now edit all builds in the Workshop. 
    • Available Robot Presets are now shown in a separate window.

Technical Adjustments

  • Improved game stability.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug where the Pilot Talent ‘Ricochet’ would specifically mention the Pilot ‘Sora Sing’.
  • Fixed a bug where the Cycle Gear ‘Minefield’ would not function properly on the Firing Range.
  • Fixed a bug where some weapons’ projectiles would spawn above or below the Robot.
  • Fixed a bug where Matriarch’s ‘Defense Fleet’ would not work against the weapon ‘Quantum’.
  • Fixed a bug where Bulwark’s Chassis module would have the maximum armor value across all levels.
  • Fixed a bug where players would be returned to the Workshop after closing a customization screen.
  • Fixed a bug where no ‘Add to Hangar’ button would be available in the Rewards screen after unlocking a Titan after a match.
  • Fixed a bug where players could reach unintended locations on the map ‘Fissure’.
  • Fixed a bug where ‘Blink’ would not work as intended if objects were in the path.
  • Fixed a bug where Power Ups would spawn below the ground in the TDM game mode.
  • Fixed a bug where the VFX of Cyclops’ ‘Death Ray’ would point in the wrong direction.
  • Fixed a bug where Armor Repair provided by a player repairing themselves would be counted towards the ‘Healing’ category in the post game statistics.
  • Fixed a bug where the VFX of Bulwark’s ‘Absorber Sphere’ would linger after the Robot was destroyed.
  • Fixed a bug where players could repeatedly dash with Anansi’s Chassis to maintain high speeds at low fuel consumption.
  • Fixed a bug where AI Bots would get stuck on the map ‘Catalyst’.
  • Fixed a bug where friends on the friend list would appear offline after they log into the game.
  • Fixed a bug where the game would automatically assign the EU region for some players.
