Take to the battlefield in Scorpion, a War Robot that deals devastating damage behind enemy lines.

COMBAT ANALYSIS

  • War Robot Model: Scorpion
  • Class: Flanker
  • Dominion: FortEvo

Calculating and creeping, Scorpion has the ability to sneak up on foes and dispatch them like a true assassin.

With a real sting in its tail, this War Robot was created to pick off stragglers with its Whiplash Core Gear. In the right hands, it can take down a whole squad of enemies, one after the other.

UNDER THE HOOD

Make the most of your War Robot by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, Supply Gear, and Cycle Gear.

Don’t forget—you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite elements! 

  • Whiplash (Core Gear): A homing projectile that deals damage on a hit, then additional damage per second to all Modules It also recharges if a target is destroyed while active.
  • Tusk (Light Weapon - Kinetic): Charge up this ballistic shotgun to gore enemies at longer range and with reduced spread.
  • Gamma Beam (Cycle Gear): Locks onto an enemy, dealing damage per second that increases based on proximity.
  • Repulsor (Supply Gear): Deals damage to enemies and pushes them and you away from each other.
