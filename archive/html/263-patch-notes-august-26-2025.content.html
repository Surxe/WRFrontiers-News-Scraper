Attention, Pilots! The following patch will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on August 26, 2025.

New Content

New Weapon: Nucleon (Heavy, Autocannon, Electromagnetic, Rare)

Nucleon is available in Daily Deals, with an increased chance of appearing.

Last Robot Standing Update

Last Robot Standing has been temporarily updated with a new ruleset providing the following power-ups:

  • Shield: Provides Shield.
  • Mass Regeneration: Regenerates the Armor of each Module.
  • Mass Armor: Increases damage resistance by 20%.
  • Mass Fuel: Increases restoration speed of fuel by 20%.
  • Mass Speed: Increases acceleration and speed by 20%.
  • Mass Ultimate: Adds Cycle Gear Charge.

Gameplay Adjustments

Armor Values

The armor values of Typhon, Siren, and Bulgasari Modules have been rounded to whole numbers.

Pursuer

Pursuer’s stats have been rebalanced:
 

Chassis

  • Acceleration has been increased from 43 to 49.
  • Maximum speed has been increased from 127 to 131.

Pelvis

  • Armor has been decreased from 61.300 to 56.000.

Legs

  • Armor has been decreased from 110.340 to 106.400.

Locust

The weapon ‘Locust’ has been rebalanced:
 

  • Damage to armor reduced from 3.800 to 3.040.
  • Damage to shields reduced from 3.400 to 1.700.
  • Time to reload increased from 2.4s to 3.0s.
  • Effective range reduced from 250m to 150m.
  • Maximum range reduced from 500m to 250m.

Tutorial Matches

The points required to win the Tutorial Matches for Beacon Rush and Team Deathmatch (TDM) have been reduced.

MLx2

Weapon behavior has been adjusted:

  • Now fires in bursts.
  • There is now a delay of 1.3s between each burst.

Spearhead Game Mode

Players now obtain spawn protection when respawning.

Crews

The ‘Ask to Join’ button has been renamed to ‘Join Crew’.

Technical Adjustments

  • Improved client stability on PS5.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug with missing and mismatching Gear Activation voice lines:
    • Added a voice line and subtitles when using Ceres’ Rejuvenator (Core Gear).
    • Added a voice line when using Suppressor (Supply Gear).
  • Fixed various bugs where Stickers would not display properly.
  • Fixed a bug where the camera would move to the right when aiming after turning off Aim Assist.
  • Fixed a bug where Global Decals were not visible on all Torsos when using the Anansi Chassis.
  • Fixed a bug where Module and Shop Bundle icons were smaller than intended.
  • Fixed a bug where players could access unintended locations on the map ‘Fissure’.
  • Fixed a bug where Stickers would not face the same direction as the Torso when trying to place Stickers.
  • Fixed a bug where the legs of the Anansi Chassis would obstruct Gear models in the Workshop.
  • Fixed a bug where a War Robot’s Torso would occasionally turn more than intended.
  • Fixed a bug where AI allied Bots would occasionally spawn in enemy spawn locations.
  • Fixed a bug where Volta (Titan) would occasionally not unlock for players, even after reaching Level 50.
