As a Wild Ten Pilot, it’s good to get to grips with your partner in battle—the War Robot. With several War Robots to choose from, each with its own unique attributes, picking the best ally for each fight is crucial.

Take the high ground with Anansi, a spider-like War Robot perfect for tactical ambushes.

COMBAT ANALYSIS

  • War Robot Model: Anansi
  • Class: Tactician 
  • Dominion: Mayflower

Arachnophobia? Never heard of her. Thanks to its high maneuverability, Anansi can quickly take the high ground and then fire while invisible to really put the scare into the enemy. 

Creating opportunities for its allies to launch attacks is Anansi’s strong suit, but it can target foes itself with homing missiles and projectiles, to bombastic effect. 

UNDER THE HOOD

Make the most of your War Robot by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, Supply Gear, and Cycle Gear.

Don’t forget, you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite elements! 

  • Camouflage Web (Core Gear): Create a web that renders all allies inside the radius invisible.
  • MLx2 (Heavy Weapon | Rocket Launcher): Use it to lock onto targets and launch a hail of homing missiles.
  • Flashbang (Cycle Gear): Fire a homing projectile that blinds enemies and deals damage.
  • Suppressor (Supply Gear): Throw a sticky emitter that reduces enemy damage in the nearby area.
