Each battlefield in War Robots: Frontiers is unique, with its own pitfalls and perks. Horizon One is no different. 

FIELD RECONNAISSANCE

The freighter ship Horizon One crashed on this barren wasteland, giving this map its name. The ship was carrying a large cargo of Noachium, the mineral that stabilizes Warp Gates. This Freecon ship is now up for grabs by any Dominion that can seize control of the map. 

Mayflower is trying to contain the environmental disaster caused by the Noachium to the habitat, FortEvo seeks to preserve and study the effects, whilst Freecon merely wants their cargo back, and to secure the crash site for themselves. 

STRATEGIC ANALYSIS

Pilots have plenty of opportunities to enrich themselves while the battle rages on on Horizon One. The map features covered routes perfect for ambushing unaware foes, with plenty of high ground for attacks and lowlands for retreats and regroups.

Pick your Drop Team carefully, and bring only the best of the best to tackle this map. Drop into Horizon One today.
