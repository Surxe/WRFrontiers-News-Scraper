Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on July 8, 2025.

New Content

  • Impact Event (July 8–15): Make a splash! Battle to earn double Impact Points from all your Assists, Kills, and Wins.

Gameplay Adjustments

  • Stranded Pilots (Job): The difficulty has been adjusted. Capturing 1 Warp Probe (Spearhead) now requires 200 Points (previously 100).
  • Sound: Added SFX for projectiles absorbed by Bulwark’s Absorber Sphere (Core Gear).
  • UI: Floating damage numbers from hitting enemies now stay on screen longer.

Bug Fixes

Technical

  • Fixed various crash-related issues on PC, PlayStation 5, and PlayStation 4.

General

  • Fixed a bug where a player could spawn in with a War Robot and be unable to control it.
  • Fixed a bug where Cyclops’ Death Ray (Core Gear) could deal damage through barriers.
  • Fixed a bug where Energy Wall (Supply Gear) would not receive any damage from explosions and splash damage.
  • Fixed a bug where Blueprints would be displayed before owned parts in the Warehouse.

Store

  • Fixed a bug where the same item could appear on the same slot multiple times in a row when the selection refreshes.
