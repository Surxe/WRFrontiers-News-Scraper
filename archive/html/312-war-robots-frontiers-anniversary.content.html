Limited-Time Mode: Infinite Fuel (March 3 – March 17)

Unstable energy readings have been detected across the Wild Ten.

A rare atmospheric anomaly is disrupting standard fuel regulators, pushing reactor systems to sustained peak output without depletion. Engineers are monitoring the phenomenon, but combat operations continue as scheduled.

Until conditions stabilize, all War Robots are operating with effectively unlimited propulsion capacity.

Adapt — or fall behind.

Gameplay Changes

Infinite Fuel enters the Unranked rotation and reshapes combat across the Wild Ten. With fuel limits removed, pilots can:

  • Boost and dash without cooldown constraints
  • Maintain extended aerial control
  • Unlock new vertical routes and attack angles

This experimental limited-time mode is designed to reimagine mobility and pacing across existing maps and modes. Its return may depend on community feedback.

New Anniversary Pilot: Bruno Lantoni

Bruno Lantoni once ruled the racing circuits with millisecond precision. When age began to erode his edge, he refused to retire. Instead, he rebuilt himself — replacing much of his body with experimental cybernetics to remain competitive.

The athletic commission banned him for “excessive modification”.

Now he brings his engineered precision to robot warfare.

Bruno Lantoni will be obtainable during the limited-time Anniversary Vault event and will become available through additional methods at a later date.

Anniversary Vault (February 24 – March 11)

As reactor anomalies ripple across the Wild Ten and combat protocols shift, corporate archives are being temporarily reopened.

To mark one year of sustained conflict, sponsors and faction backers have authorized limited access to secured vault inventories — releasing select pilots, wraps, and archived assets back into circulation.

Among them is Bruno Lantoni, newly cleared for deployment in the Wild Ten.
