Attention, Pilots! The following patch for War Robots: Frontiers will be deployed after a short maintenance period on April 14, 2026.

New Content

  • New Battle Pass: Mirror Threat (April 14 – May 18)
  • New War Robot: Garuda
  • New Weapon: I.O.N.

Balance Changes

Slugger

  • Armor Damage decreased from 2,200 to 1,800
  • Shield Damage decreased from 1,800 to 1,550

Scatter

  • Armor Damage decreased from 7,800 to 6,800
  • Shield Damage decreased from 4,250 to 3,850

Ares

  • Wrath Active Duration decreased from 8s to 7s
  • Cooldown increased from 35s to 40s

Purifier Torso

  • Armor decreased from 160,000 to 144,000

Purifier Shoulder

  • Armor decreased from 170,200 to 147,000

Purifier Chassis

  • Pelvic Armor decreased from 152,200 to 144,000
  • Leg Armor decreased from 152,200 to 144,000

Kumo Shoulder

  • Armor decreased from 141,100 to 132,000

Lancelot Shoulder

  • Armor decreased from 183,200 to 156,000

Bulwark Shoulder

  • Armor decreased from 186,700 to 168,000

Raven Shoulder

  • Armor decreased from 86,600 to 82,000
  • Shield Amount increased from 21,600 to 22,200

Anansi Chassis

  • Acceleration decreased from 54 to 52
  • Max Speed decreased from 97 to 89

Conflux

  • Armor Damage increased from 2,300 to 2,500

Skuld

  • Armor Damage decreased from 1,275 to 1,150

Nucleon

  • Armor Damage decreased from 1,050 to 950
  • Shield Damage decreased from 1,330 to 1,250

Hefty

  • Armor Damage decreased from 1,275 to 1,200
  • Shield Damage decreased from 950 to 850

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug that unintentionally nerfed Volta; its stats are now correct.
  • Fixed the reload animation for the Coriolis weapon on the Norna Titan.
  • The push force of the Repulsor Gear now scales with level and also affects the player’s own War Robot.
  • Reduced the brightness of the Alpha Titan’s Sticky Sensor visual effect.
  • Fixed a bug where, in certain cases, interrupting Crix’s Core Gear caused the War Robot to dash endlessly.
  • Conflux for the Volta Titan can now be disassembled for Salvage.
  • Improved bot behavior on the Tortuga map.
  • To comply with updated Sony PlayStation requirements, the Friends tab on PlayStation™Network has been renamed to PlayStation.
  • Fixed a bug with the Napalm Shell (Harpy's Core Gear): damage now correctly applies only to modules that have come into contact with the effect, and does not affect unaffected modules.
