Incoming, Pilots! A new major update will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on June 17, 2025. This update will introduce Season 2: Power Surge, and a host of new content and quality-of-life updates.

New Content

Season 2: Power Surge

The battle for Terminus continues. Despite the ongoing blockade, Pilots have adapted and found new sources of supplies. Some have learned to scavenge and build, while others have turned to smuggling. But just as life on Tortuga seems to be getting back on track, the Warp network collapses, paralyzing all activities in the Wild Ten. With everything at stake, it’s up to the Pilots to fix what's broken.

War Robots: Loki & Mesa

  • Loki (Unlockable via the Scavenger’s Hunt Battle Pass & Daily Deals): Trickster by name, trickster by nature. Loki's ability to use holographic projections makes it an excellent infiltrator and scout. Its armor is strong for its class, and its anti-shield firepower means it can easily take on flankers, though it should avoid encounters with heavier foes. Add Loki to your Drop Team and see what this stealthy War Robot can do. 
  • Mesa (Unlockable via the Spark of Ingenuity Battle Pass & Daily Deals from July 29): Defend ‘til the end with Mesa, a strong defender with amazing ally-protecting abilities. Deploy barriers to shield Mesa and its teammates from projectiles, or unleash a biotech drone that follows alongside it, buffing allies and weakening enemies in the area. 

Titan: Volta

  • Volta (Unlockable via account progression at Level 50): Turn up the voltage. This live wire Titan is the first quadruped to be built on Tortuga, and it’s as aggressive as it is electrifying. With its ability to double jump and hover, these new traversal capabilities are sure to set Volta apart from other Titans on the battlefield. Activate its electric weapon, generate a moving barrier, or block projectiles and deal damage to enemies foolish enough to pass through.

Game Mode: Spearhead

Powerslide into a brand-new game mode: Spearhead. 

Two teams fight over randomly spawning Warp Probes to stabilize Warp in the region. Locate and capture them first to earn Points! Hit the Points cap, stay ahead, or eliminate all enemies to win.

Battle Pass: Scavenger’s Haul

Whether you’re on the Free track or want to upgrade your Battle Pass to earn more rewards, dozens of adrenaline-pumping rewards are waiting to be unlocked:

  • Loki Modules
  • Halo Weapons
  • Recon Mode (Cycle Gear)
  • Fuel Burn (Supply Gear)
  • 5 Stickers
  • 5 Paints
  • 2 Decals
  • 2 Wear and Tears
  • 1 Common Pilot
  • Intel, Credits & Salvage

In Season 1: Blockade, new Shipments for the Battle Pass were added regularly, unlocking access to new pages of rewards as they arrived. Starting with Season 2: Power Surge, all Battle Pass rewards will be available to unlock from day one. Earn those Battle Tokens to be among the first Pilots to construct Loki!

Additionally, Intel can now be obtained from the Battle Pass for free. You can earn up to 200 Intel through free progression, a crucial resource used to upgrade your best Builds.

We’ve also rebalanced the Battle Pass and Free progression to give you even more Credits, Salvage, and Intel throughout the Pass. You can still complete at least one full Shipment (plus change) without upgrading your Pass, but Free progression will not award enough Battle Tokens to obtain the Torso or Chassis Modules on Shipment 2 and beyond. However, keep an eye on Daily Deals, and you can spend your Credits on the Modules and Weapons you seek!

A new Battle Pass will launch on July 29: Spark of Ingenuity. Be sure to collect all your rewards in Scavenger’s Haul before this date!

Battle Pass Content Available in Daily Deals

War Robots and Weapons will now launch in Daily Deals at the same time they’re available in the Battle Pass! This change is designed to give Pilots more options for unlocking battle gear, without having to spend WarpReals.

With the launch of Season 2: Power Surge, Loki Modules and the Halo Weapon are now available in Daily Deals, along with Scorpion Modules and the Tusk Weapon from Season 1: Blockade.

Hero Pilot: Sora Sing

Unleash the sparky Sora Sing (available in the Store) as your War Robot’s new Pilot. This infamous femme fatale has found herself with a public persona she may not deserve, taking the fall for war crimes which saw her stripped of her citizenship, and expatriated to the Wild Ten—the only place that would take her. 

While her fellow Pilots may not trust her just yet, her talents hit all the high notes, and her ability to reload faster and boost damage (among others) make her a more than capable Pilot. Remember, it’s not over until the Sora sings…

Balancing Adjustments

General

  • Destroying a Titan now yields 60% less Cycle Gear Charge Points.
  • Destroying a Titan in Warp Rush and Last Robot Standing now yields 45% less Titan Charge Points.
  • Destroying a Titan in Team Deathmatch (TDM) now yields 10% less Titan Charge Points. Destroying a Robot now yields 11% more Titan Charge Points.
  • Increased the amount of Titan Charge Points earned when your last War Robot is destroyed.

Gear

Minefield (Cycle Gear)

  • Mines now spawn below the player who activates the Gear.
  • Mines that are deployed are invisible to enemy players.
  • A mine detonates when an enemy War Robot steps on it, dealing damage over an area and slowing the enemy down. The slowing effect does not increase on repeated mine detonations.
  • The mine spread has been reduced. War Robots can no longer pass through the minefield without triggering an explosion.

General Adjustments

Matchmaking

  • Previously, players were matched based on their win rate. With this update, we will base it first on League Points, then on win rate to ensure more evenly balanced matchmaking.
  • We have added an unranked matchmaking group for players at Level 7–14. Players will be matched with Bronze League starting at Level 15.

Leagues

League placements will be reset, and ranks will be adjusted for Season 2:

  • Players ranked in Silver will start in Bronze.
  • Players ranked in Gold will start in Silver.
  • Players ranked in Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, or Noachium will start in Gold.

League Points will be set according to your new League placement:

  • Bronze = 0 League Points
  • Silver = 300 League Points
  • Gold = 600 League Points

Additionally, the amount of League Points needed to reach Noachium has been increased to 4,000 League Points.

Store

  • The Store now includes a section to display the chances for items to appear in Daily Deals.
  • Added a dynamic cost to refreshing Daily Deals, along with the first refresh being in Credits. The refreshing cost resets daily.
    • First Refresh: 700 Credits
    • Second Refresh: 15 WarpReals
    • Third Refresh: 25 WarpReals
    • Fourth Refresh: 35 WarpReals
    • Fifth Refresh and above: 45 WarpReals
  • Players will now be redirected to the Store when attempting to purchase an item with insufficient currency.

Hangar, Workshop & Warehouse

  • Filtering/sorting options will now persist after crafting or upgrading an item.
  • Newly acquired items will now include a “NEW” icon.
  • Added a hint message under the War Robot that provides information about why it can’t be assembled.
  • Added a “Disassemble” button to Factory Builds.
  • Added a “Go Back to Editing” button that appears when removing a War Robot from the Hangar. This allows players to continue working on a Build instead of being forced to revert or save the changes.
  • Added acquisition details for Modules.
  • Added an “Install to Hangar” button when editing a Robot Build.
  • Improved the camera behavior in the Workshop and Warehouse.
  • Navigation lines pointing to modules when building a War Robot have been improved in the workshop.

Player Progression
