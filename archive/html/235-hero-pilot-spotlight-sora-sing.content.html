Pilots are the final puzzle piece in your loadout, and choosing the ideal companion for your War Robot is critical to your success…or failure. 

Raise your voice and welcome Sora Sing to the fray, an infamous criminal making a name for herself on the Wild Ten.

BACKGROUND ANALYSIS

  • Name: Sora Sing
  • Class: Flanker
  • Dominion: Mayflower

Sora’s arrival to the Wild Ten was no accident—it was a punishment. Stripped of her citizenship and expatriated, Sora was sent to the only place that would take her. However, her story is not all it seems. 

Sora began her career as a military cadet, expelled for cheating and barred from Mayflower’s brigades. Seeking employment outside her Dominion, she found a home in the paramilitary unit known as the Cohoes Battalion. She quickly rose through the ranks and played a crucial role in crushing the Founders Rebellion, an extremist uprising that saw fighting in several Mayflower colonies. 

Sora was the face of the Cohoes Battalion, and when the unit was accused of extrajudicial murders, she was the one to take the fall. In fact, Sora was one of only a few Battalion members who were not willing to execute POWs—and it was for that reason she was thrown under the bus by her commanding officers, and now begrudgingly takes her place as a Pilot.

TACTICAL TALENTS

Every Pilot has Talents, but it’s up to you to pick the best ones for battle. Pick one Talent per Rank, and remember—it’s not over until Sora Sings…

Rank 1: General Talents

  • Gearhead: Gear goes off cooldown faster.
  • Case-Hardened: You take less damage from all sources.
  • Feed Hacker: Your weapons reload quicker.

Rank 2: Flanker Talents (Class Specific)

  • Gun Nut: Your shotguns and special weapons deal more damage.
  • Shields Expert: Boosts shield.
  • Fast Reflexes: Boosts primary and secondary stats of Movement Gear.

Rank 3: Volatile Talents (Personality Specific)

  • Reactive Rage: If any of your Modules are missing more than half its armor, your weapons deal more damage.
  • Chemical Hazard: All corrosive and fire effects deal more damage and last longer.
  • Hellraiser: Destroying enemies boost your blast damage for a short time.

Rank 4: Mayflower Talents (Dominion Specific)

  • Red Alert: Taking damage speeds up your weapon reload
  • Survial Instict: If any Module has less than 30% armor, you take less damage from all sources.
  • Quick Reflexes: Activating any defensive Gear reduces shield cooldown.

Rank 5: Ace Talent 

Sora acquired this Talent by hitting all the high notes.

  • Ricochet: Your shots have a small chance to hit all of the target’s Modules at once.
