With the December 23 update, the War Robots Kumo and Anansi will be added to the progression. Players who reach the required levels will now be able to craft these War Robot Spiders.

Here’s when you can unlock their modules:

Anansi

  • Level 173 - Anansi Shoulder
  • Level 176 - Anansi Chassis
  • Level 179 - Suppressor Supply Gear
  • Level 182 - MLX2 Weapon
  • Level 185 - Anansi Torso

Kumo

  • Level 188 - Kumo Shoulder
  • Level 191 - Kumo Chassis
  • Level 197 - Horde Weapon
  • Level 200 - Kumo Torso
