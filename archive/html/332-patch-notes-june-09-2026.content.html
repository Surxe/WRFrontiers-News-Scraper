Attention, Pilots! The following update for War Robots: Frontiers will be deployed after a short maintenance period on June 9, 2026.

New Content

  • New Battle Pass: Lockdown Dash
  • New War Robot: Wyrm
  • New Weapon: Aftershock
  • New Common pilots: "Hornet" Cross, Iris

New Battle Pass: Lockdown Dash (June 9 – June 23)

Unlock the Flanker robot Garuda, Orkan and I.O.N. weapons as well as valuable resources and new cosmetics!

New War Robot: Wyrm

Wyrm pulls and grinds down enemy lines, converting bulky armor and three heavy hardpoints into front-line domination.

Starting June 9, Wyrm will be available through Daily Deals and direct offers.

New Robot Weapon: Aftershock

Heavy electromagnetic weapon built for short, intense close-range engagements

New Common Pilots: "Hornet" Cross, Iris

Two new common pilots join the team!

New Event

Intel and Salvage Discount Event (June 9 – 16)

This week, the Tortuga engineers invite Pilots to take a closer look at the following builds:

  • War Robot Modules: Phantom, Griffin, Cyclops
  • Weapons: Gemini, Lighter, Scrubber, Quantum
  • Gear: Umbrella, Holofield, Minefield, Homing Missile, Blink, Energy Wall

Player Progression

Player account progression has been expanded. The maximum player level has been increased to 260.

New crafting unlocks:

  • Level 257: Iron Veil Cycle Gear
  • Level 260: Zenit Weapon 

Matchmaking Update: Road to Ranked

Players level 15 and below queueing for the Road to Ranked mode will now be grouped into a full 6-player team and face off against bots. Matchmaking rules for all other game modes remain unchanged.
