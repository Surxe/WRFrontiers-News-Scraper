Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on July 31, 2025.

Battle Pass Adjustments

  • Players working through the Free Battle Pass can now earn enough Tokens to access all 4 Shipments of the Spark of Ingenuity Battle Pass, with some items still locked for Battle Pass Gold players.

Gameplay Adjustments

  • Matchmaking now prioritizes Crews of 4 or more to only be able to play with other Crews of the same size. This change is to protect solo-queue players from playing against full organised groups, and allows organised groups to match together easier.
  • Maximum matchmaking time for Crews consisting of 4 or more players has been changed to 5 minutes.

Bug Fixes

  • To correctly allow all players to unlock Shipment 4, some Shipment 3 items have been moved from Battle Pass Gold to the Free Battle Pass.
  • Fixed several crash related issues on PlayStation®5.
  • Fixed a bug where ‘Homing Missile’ would deal more damage than intended.
  • Fixed a bug where the system would attempt to sell the last highlighted item in the Warehouse instead of the selected item.
  • Fixed a bug where the Premium Account Status could not be activated while in the ‘Now Unlocking’ page.
