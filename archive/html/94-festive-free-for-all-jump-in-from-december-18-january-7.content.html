Enter the world of War Robots: Frontiers and play for free for three whole weeks!All players can experience the full Early Access version of the game via MY.GAMES Launcher or the Steam Demo Version, even if they don’t own a Pioneer Pack.

Check out what’s new in the recent Autumn Update, become a Pilot, assemble your mechs, and play the following special events for free from December 18 until January 7:

  • Warlords Of Winter (Dec 11–31): Earn Honor alongside your fellow Pilots to obtain cool rewards. Work together and check your daily progress to see how close you are to the next reward. 
  • Bulwark Event (Dec 18–24): Build a Bulwark to surpass all others! Play one match daily to collect progressive rewards and assemble a complete ferocious Robot build.
  • Holiday Event (Dec 25–31): Play 10, 20, and 25 matches to earn festive gifts like Lighter and Zeus Weapons and a Dazzle Ability. Spread cheer to all the Robots in your Hangar with these merry rewards!
  • Ares Event (Jan 1–7): Pull together an astounding Ares build by playing one battle daily and collecting parts.

If you decide to take up residence in the Wild Ten, grab a Pioneer Pack, which grants full Early Access to War Robots: Frontiers via wrfrontiers.com and Steam.

IMPORTANT: Rewards are subject to a progress wipe when Open Beta begins.
