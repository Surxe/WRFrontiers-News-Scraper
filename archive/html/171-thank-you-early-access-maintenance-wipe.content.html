Past & Present: Your Feedback, Your Playtime, Your Changes

Over the course of Early Access, the team has been hard at work improving the mechanics of the game and balancing gameplay, to the betterment of the overall player experience. 

These are just some of the changes and improvements we've made thanks to your support and feedback:

UI/UX Overhauls

We’ve redesigned the Hangar and Workshop to make it easy and intuitive to construct War Robots and optimize your Drop Team for battle. We hope players enjoy making and trying out lots of unique builds. The UI/UX team also deployed a large update in January to improve the clarity, usability, and intuitiveness of interfaces, features, and the overall player experience. We are still actively listening to feedback from players and will pay close attention to this part of the game, adjusting it if necessary. 

“We’re grateful for how our team worked together to rework the entire user interface, introducing a fresh new style and making meaningful improvements to the Robot construction experience,” says Parisa Zhong, UI/UX Director for War Robots: Frontiers.

Reworked Map Layouts & Visuals

A thrilling tactical experience is at the heart of what we’re trying to deliver with War Robots: Frontiers. Thanks to the help of our intrepid Early Access Pilots, we identified and developed opportunities and metas to ensure every battle is unique, fair, and rich with tactical possibilities.

Improved Robot Movement & Animations

We want Pilots to feel the weight and mechanical power of War Robots at their fingertips. Early Access gave us the time and feedback we needed to finetune the look and feel of piloting for maximum immersion.

New Social Features

Coordinating with your team in War Robots: Frontiers is critical and could make all the difference between victory and defeat. The addition of Friend Lists, voice chat, and in-battle pings broadens your ability to communicate, put together a team, and work on your tactics on the fly. We can’t wait to see your squads in action!

There’s so much more that we can barely cover it all, from balancing updates to all the new content and weapons you’ll see in the release version! What’s your favorite addition or improvement to War Robots: Frontiers so far? Let us know on Discord, because your feedback will continue to shape the game in the future, too!

Maintenance & Early Access Ends

Pioneer Packs will no longer be sold as of February 28, 2025 at 19:00 CET. Players who already own a Pioneer Pack can continue to play War Robots: Frontiers until maintenance begins.

  • Maintenance Start: March 1, 10:00 CET
  • Maintenance End: March 4, 19:00 CET

A new update will be required on PC. Players on PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, and Xbox One who previously played a Playtest must download the new version of the game from March 4, 2025.

Early Access Progress Wipe

To ensure all new Pilots get the chance to start on a level playing field with Early Access players, all forms of in-game progression from the Early Access period (including Playtests) will be wiped according to previous communications. 

Certain items purchased, earned or received during Early Access will be available to you in the launch version of War Robots: Frontiers.

Pioneer Packs

Pioneer Pack owners will retain the items received from whichever Packs they purchased, including the Varangian Sticker, Orkans, Varangian (War Robot), WarpReals, Sinclair Decal, Kate Sinclair (Hero Pilot), Battle Passes, and Premium Account.
