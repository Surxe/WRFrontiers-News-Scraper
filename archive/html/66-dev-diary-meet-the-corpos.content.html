The Meet The Corpos Update arrives in War Robots: Frontiers on June 28, and it’s time to say hello to the upcoming content in a new Dev Diary.

  • Prepare to take on Daily and Weekly Contracts to earn a new currency (Intel) for item upgrades. Alloys are still needed to level up your gear, but getting past certain levels will now require Intel as well.
  • You can earn rewards with the Supply Line—collect a special currency from battles then spend it on seasonal rewards, including the newest items, cosmetics, and other goods direct from Corpo warehouses.
  • Reap what you sow with the new Titan—Grim! Tear through enemies with this aggressive and speedy bot, exchanging durability for damage.

Check out a sneak peek of all the content arriving with the Meet the Corpos Update in the latest Dev Diary from Zukata, the Creative Director of War Robots: Frontiers.
