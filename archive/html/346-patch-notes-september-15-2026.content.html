New Event Pass: Shadowed Bastion (September 15 – October 5)

Unlock the latest robot Angler, Relic Lancelot and Relic Thunders as well as various resources and new cosmetics!

New War Robot: Angler

Backline Tactician designed for fire support and squad defense. Equipped with the Tesla Feed ability, Angler accelerates shield recovery for frontline allies and zaps foes surrounding them with bonus damage—all while staying safely out of the crossfire. Though its chassis boasts extra load capacity for heavy long-range setups, its lack of direct brawling durability means you must rely on strategic positioning.

Starting September 15, Angler will be available through Daily Deals, direct offers and the new Event pass.

New Robot Weapon: Breacher

Mid-range weapon firing homing projectiles with area-of-effect damage. While fast close-range skirmishers can outmaneuver its tracking, Breacher is devastating against slow-moving targets—forcing them to burn fuel dodging constant splash damage.

Starting September 15, Breacher will be available through Daily Deals and direct offers. It might be featured in a Battle Pass or Event Pass later.

Bugfixes and various changes

  • Improved optimization.
  • Improved hit tracking.
  • Improved the AI of bots in Quantum Core game mode.
  • Changed Core and Spawn locations in Quantum Core to balance the win rate between the opposing teams.
  • Enhanced bot navigation in all modes and fixed some problematic areas on the maps. 
  • Fixed a bug where a piston was missing from one of Relic Scorpion's legs.
  • Fixed a bug where using blink abilities would forcibly rotate the camera toward the direction of blink.
  • Fixed a bug where capturing beacons in Quantum Core did not count toward quest progress.
  • Fixed a bug where the frame rate option in the settings failed to toggle and did not display the FPS counter.
