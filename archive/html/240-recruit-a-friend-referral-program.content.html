Tutorial Job Rewards

As your friend progresses through the Tutorial Jobs, you’ll receive +5% bonus Credits (on top of the Credits earned from the Job), and they’ll receive +25% bonus Credits.

When the referee completes all 24 Tutorial Jobs, they will officially be a Pilot, and new rewards will unlock for the referrer (you!).
