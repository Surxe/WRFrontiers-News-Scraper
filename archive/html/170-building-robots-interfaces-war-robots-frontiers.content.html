Eyes up, Pilots! You already know how to build a War Robot. Next, let’s explore some of the interfaces you can find via the main menu in-game so you can hit the battlefield with the perfect War Robot. 

There are three places you need to get familiar with—Hangar, Workshop, and Warehouse. In each of these, you can create Robot Builds, store Modules, and scrap unwanted equipment to obtain Salvage. 

HANGAR

The Hangar is where your active Robots are stored between intense battles. Your Drop Team—made up of six Robots (including a Titan)—can also be found here. 

Add or remove War Robots—including battle-ready Factory Builds and Custom Builds—at your leisure to optimize your Drop Team!

WORKSHOP

The Workshop is the hub of your building activity. This is where you craft your Custom Build Robot using Modules (such as Torsos, Chassis, Shoulders, etc.), craft new Modules, or scrap unwanted equipment to earn Salvage (which you can then turn into new parts and upgrades).

This is also where you add Gear (equipment that unlocks tactical abilities) to your Robots, including Cycle, Supply, and Core Gear. 

Cycle Gear is a Module that has a cooldown on its abilities. Supply Gears offer consumable abilities. Core Gears are more unique, and come permanently fitted in a War Robot’s Torso Module. 

This is also where you can inject personality into your Robot by adding cosmetic items like Paints, Wraps, Decals, Stickers, and Wear & Tear to jazz up your war machine. 

WAREHOUSE

The Warehouse is where you store spare Modules, Pilots, and rewards you’ve earned—think of this as your inventory. Browse through Weapons and Pilot Talents at your leisure to plan your next perfect Build.

Now you know how and where to build your dream Drop Team—what are you waiting for, Pilot?
