On the Wild Ten, a War Robot can only get you so far. Pilots need another trick up their sleeve to achieve victory in battle—the Titan. Choose your best asset from four Titans, each with their own special talents.

Vault into battle with Volta, a Titan that thrives on electrifying action.

COMBAT ANALYSIS

  • Titan Model: Volta
  • Class: Assault
  • Dominion: Freecon

Once part of the Freecon security detail, Volta was defeated by rebels during Tortuga’s Uprising, a worker rebellion which sought to claim independence from the dominion that owned the mining outpost now known as Tortuga Space Station

As an act of defiance, The Lace’s team has restored Volta to fight on Tortuga’s side.

As an Assault Titan, Volta completes the set of Titan powers, along with Matriarch on defence, Alpha as a key tactician, and Grim as a powerful flanker.

Volta is designed for frontal attacks, carrying a powerful weapon, and equipped with lots of tools that allow it to outplay and outmaneuver the enemy.

Volta’s pièce de résistance is its traversal. Not only is it the first quadruped Titan, but it is able to double jump. This Titan truly has the jump on the competition.

UNDER THE HOOD

  • Short Circuit (Titan Gear): Releases a static discharge that bounces off to nearby targets. It deals damage and prevents enemies from using Gear.
  • Galvanic Screen (Titan Gear): Generates a barrier that blocks projectiles and slowly travels forward. All enemies passing through it receive damage.
  • Tesla Coil (Titan Gear): Activates a built-in electric weapon that automatically picks a target near your crosshair, dealing damage per shot to a random Module.
