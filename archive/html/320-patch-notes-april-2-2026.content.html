Attention, Pilots! The following patch for War Robots: Frontiers will be deployed after a short maintenance period on March 24, 2026.

Gameplay Adjustments

  • Improved automatic matchmaking region selection. The game now performs more frequent ping checks across regions to determine the best server for each player. This should address the issues reported by players experiencing higher-than-normal ping.
  • While we continue to investigate the ping issues recently reported by PlayStation players, the region selection option has been temporarily re-enabled in the settings.

Balance Changes

Ecdysis (Heike Core Gear)

  • Damage to shields increased from 22,000 to 35,000. This change improves Heike’s effectiveness when paired with defensive or assault shoulders, while maintaining its intended role and preventing it from becoming an overly versatile flanker.
  • Cooldown behavior at max level has been adjusted:
    • Cooldown reduced from 40s to 32s.
    • The cooldown timer now begins after the ability ends (instead of on activation).
    • Cooldown progression at lower levels has been rebalanced accordingly.

Bug Fixes

  • The active duration of Ecdysis (Heike Core Gear) is now correctly displayed on the HUD.
  • Fixed a bug where damage dealt to enemy shields by Ecdysis (Heike Core Gear) was not displayed.
  • Fixed an issue where the effective range of Ecdysis (Heike Core Gear) was incorrect.
  • Fixed a visual camera glitch on the Heike torso in the Workshop during customization.
  • Fixed missing localization in the descriptions of Kinetic Barrier and Ion Volley for the Norna Titan.
  • Fixed a bug where the Death Ray (Cyclops Core Gear) could deal incorrect damage.
  • Fixed a bug where decals placed on Crix and Decker War Robots appeared incorrectly oriented.
  • Fixed an issue where, if no Supply Gear was equipped, pressing the Supply button would instead trigger Cycle Gear.
