Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be made to War Robots: Frontiers (Early Access), which will be available following a short period of maintenance on April 3, 2024. 

Squads

  • Inviting and adding friends to squads now works correctly.
  • All statuses of squad members now display correctly.
  • Fixed a bug that allowed players to be invited to a squad twice.

Robots

  • Cosmetic items (Decals, Stickers, etc) positions are now saved after rebooting the game.
  • Hangar warnings about incomplete builds have been improved and now indicate when Blueprints are equipped.
  • Fixed a bug that caused some Robot Parts to be missing on the Spawn Point mini-map. 

Weapons and Abilities 

  • Camera shake has been improved when firing Shredder, Glory, Gozer, and Zeus Weapons.
  • The Infinity Ammo Ability has received the following changes:
    • Lvl 1 - Duration reduced from 4.89 to 4.42
    • Lvl 2 - Duration reduced from 4.89 to 4.65
    • Lvl 3 - Duration reduced from 5.15 to 4.89
    • Lvl 7 - Duration increased from 5.42 to 5.7
    • Lvl 8 - Duration increased from 5.7 to 6.
    • Lvl 9 - Duration increased from 5.7 to 6.
    • Lvl 10 - Duration increased from 5.7 to 6.32.
    • Lvl 11 - Duration increased from 6 to 6.65.
    • Lvl 12 - Duration increased from 6 to 7.
    • Lvl 13 - Duration increased from 6 to 7.
  • The Regeneration Ability has received the following changes: 
    • Lvl 1 - Regeneration speed reduced from 3750 to 3365.8 and duration reduced from 6.86 to 5.85.
    • Lvl 2 - Regeneration speed reduced from 3947 to 3739.8 and duration reduced from 6.86 to 5.85.
    • Lvl 3 - Regeneration speed reduced from 3947 to 3739.8 and duration reduced from 7.22 to 6.16.
    • Lvl 4 - Regeneration speed increased from 3947 to 4155.3, duration reduced from 7.22 to 6.16, and cooldown increased from 50 to 60.
    • Lvl 5 - Regeneration speed increased from 4155 to 4155.3, and duration reduced from 7.22 to 6.16.
    • Lvl 6 - Regeneration speed increased from 4,155 to 4,155.3, and duration reduced from 7.6 to 6.84.
    • Lvl 7 - Duration reduced from 7.6 to 6.84.
    • Lvl 8 - Cooldown increased from 42 to 50.

Pilots 

New Pilot Talents for the Flanker, Tank, Brawler, and Range Support Classes added:

Brawler Talents:

  • Autocannon Weaponry - Increases damage for machine guns.
  • Shotgun Weaponry - Increases damage for shotguns.
  • Attack Mastery - Improves the effectiveness of attacking abilities.

Ranged Support Talents:

  • Ranged Weaponry - Increases the damage of sniper and artillery weapons.
  • Defensive Mastery - Increases the effectiveness of defensive abilities.
  • Support mastery - Increases the effectiveness of support abilities.

Tank Talents:

  • Sustain Weaponry - Increases weapon damage for Sustain guns.
  • Defensive Mastery - Increases the effectiveness of defensive abilities.
  • Attack Mastery - Increases the effectiveness of attacking abilities.

Flanker Talents:

  • Rocket Weaponry - Increases damage for rocket launchers.
  • Special Weaponry - Increases damage for special guns.
  • Movement Mastery - Increases the efficiency of movement abilities.

Additionally, the Pilot Experience bar now displays correctly when the max level is reached.

UI Improvements

  • The Battle Pass purchase screen now shows the correct amount of Battle Tokens that will be received after the purchase.
  • Fixed a bug where after purchasing a Battle Pass on the post-combat screen, the purchase button did not disappear.
  • Multiple versions of the same customization can no longer be incorrectly purchased via Offers.
  • Fixed the “Daily Deals” text not displaying correctly in some languages. 
  • Changed the name of the servers to the region name.

Map Fixes

  • Fissure: Rooftop textures now display correctly.

Technical Fixes

  • The server with the lowest ping will be selected automatically when launching the game for the first time.
