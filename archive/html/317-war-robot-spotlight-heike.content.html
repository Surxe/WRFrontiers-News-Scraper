Combat Analysis

  • War Robot Model: Heike
  • Class: Defender
  • Dominion: FortEvo

Heike is a mobile Defender designed for high-pressure frontline assaults. While it carries the durability expected of its class, it distinguishes itself with the ability to advance alongside allied units rather than holding a static position.

Engineered for dense urban environments and heavily built-up combat zones, Heike excels where fights are tight, chaotic, and unforgiving. Its kit rewards decisive action—pushing into contested areas, disrupting enemy defenses, and creating openings for teammates.

However, Heike depends on coordination. It performs best when supported by allies ready to commit to aggressive plays and capitalize on its momentum.

Under the Hood

Make the most of your War Robot by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, Supply Gear, and Cycle Gear.

Don’t forget, you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite modules!

  • Ecdysis (Core Gear): Discharges its shields in a 70m pulse, dealing 22,000 to enemy shields and granting 30% damage resistance to Heike and 15% to allies within the area for 8s
  • Thunder (Kinetic, Shotgun, Heavy): Heavy shotgun with a wide pellet spread. Brutal at close range.
  • Ghost Turret (Supply Gear): Deploys a turret that attacks the closest enemy for 850 damage per shot over 13s.
  • Gamma Beam (Cycle Gear): Locks onto the enemy for 9s, dealing up to 19,000 damage per second. Damage scales with proximity.

Stats shown are for max level, factory build. Stats are subject to change pending future balancing updates.
