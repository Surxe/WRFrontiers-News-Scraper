War Robots: Frontiers now supports Twitch Drops, a great way to earn special in-game content while watching your favorite streamers on Twitch!

Watch any Drop-enabled Twitch stream between December 9 (12:00 UTC) and January 5 (12:00 UTC), rack up two hours of cumulative viewing time, then claim a brand-new Weapon for your War Robots: Scourge.

Your rewards will appear in your Drops Inventory when you’ve successfully earned them. Remember to claim it, and make sure your account is linked (scroll down for details). Even if you’re not playing in Early Access, you can claim the reward now and save it for later. Think of it as scoping out the competition!

HOW TO LINK YOUR TWITCH AND WAR ROBOTS: FRONTIERS ACCOUNTS

To claim your items, you must link your War Robots: Frontiers account with your Twitch account:
 

  • Log in here (use your Steam or VK Play account if that’s how you play the game).
  • Make sure you log in with your correct established account. If you register a new account, rewards will automatically be assigned to that account.
  • Select ‘Link with Twitch’, and make sure you confirm the connection.

WHERE TO WATCH WAR ROBOTS: FRONTIERS ON TWITCH

Check out the War Robots: Frontiers game directory on Twitch and be on the lookout for streams with Drops in their titles!

HOW TO ENABLE WAR ROBOTS: FRONTIERS DROPS ON TWITCH

Any streamer playing War Robots: Frontiers can participate in this Twitch Drops campaign! If you want to enable Twitch Drops on your channel, all you need to do is: link your War Robots: Frontiers and Twitch accounts, enable Drops in your Creator Dashboard on Twitch (do this in ‘Viewer Rewards’ from your channel settings), and then start streaming War Robots: Frontiers to your audience!
