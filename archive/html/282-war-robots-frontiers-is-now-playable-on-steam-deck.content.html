War Robots: Frontiers players on Steam can now take their battles on the go with the portable Steam Deck! The game has achieved “Playable” status, meaning Steam players can pilot their War Robots anywhere. Simply find the game in your Steam Library to start playing. 

While it’s not yet fully “Verified”, you might encounter minor UI issues, such as:

  • Entering text may require manually invoking the on-screen keyboard
  • Some in-game text is small and may be difficult to read

For more about the Steam Deck, visit their official website, or get help at Steam Support. Get ready to take your War Robots: Frontiers battles anywhere—the Wild Ten is waiting for you!
