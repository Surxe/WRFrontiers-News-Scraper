Hand the controls over to Carmen, a confident Pilot with undeniable skills. Her Ace Talent, Press The Attack, boosts rate of fire when damaging an enemy with your Gear.

Equip your Defender War Robots with a sticky grenade that weakens nearby foes.

Show off your fighting spirit with this dynamic style.

Technical Adjustments

  • US servers have been split into three separate servers:
    • US East
    • US Central
    • US West
  • Improved stability for the PS5 client.

Gameplay Adjustments

Leaver Penalty

To reduce the number of players who leave matches prematurely, match rewards will no longer be distributed to players who leave a match before a certain point.

Chassis Upgrades

The upgrade system for all War Robot Chassis in the game, including Titans, has been reworked and rebalanced.

Previously, some Chassis only upgraded Acceleration, while others only upgraded Max Speed. Now all Chassis receive upgrades to both stats.

Most Chassis retain the same stats as before at level 5, except for those with very low Acceleration. The new minimum Acceleration in the game is now set to 30, and has been assigned to Bulwark ( previously 27). Other low-Acceleration Chassis received similar or slightly smaller increases depending on their original values.

The Armor gained per level has been significantly increased, and total Armor at max level has also been slightly raised.

Workshop

  • The Info Panel in the Workshop for both War Robots and Titans has been updated.
    • The stats now update automatically when switching between Modules.
    • Differences between changes are now visually highlighted.
    • The layout has been improved for better readability.
  • Factory Wraps can now be customized with customization options such as Decals, Materials, Stickers, and Weathering.
    • A Revert function has also been implemented for Factory Wraps, restoring the Wrap to its factory default state.
  • The maximum Sticker size has been increased.

Crews

  • Players can now request to join Crews via their Friend List.
  • Switching to other screens while the status of a Crew is set to ‘Ready’ may not allow the players to join the match. To prevent this, players in a Crew who set their status to ‘Ready’ will now have their UI disabled. Players can set their status back to ‘Not Ready’ by pressing the ‘Play’ button again.

Balance Changes

Fuel Burn (Supply Gear)

  • Fuel Burn has had its mechanics slightly reworked.
  • After using Fuel Burn, Fuel Regeneration is now blocked for 5 seconds.
  • Upgrading Fuel Burn no longer increases its range. The range is now set to 375 meters across all levels.
  • Ability Charges have been reduced from 6 to 4.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug where Ceres could not jump as high as intended.
  • Fixed a bug where the arrow icons to scroll through War Robot Builds in the Workshop would not display when there are 7 Builds.
  • Fixed a bug where, after fully unlocking a War Robot via progression tracking, the system would not automatically track the next War Robot to unlock.
  • Fixed a bug where the ‘Track Item Unlock’ button was displayed for Titans in the Rewards track.
  • Fixed a bug where players were unable to assign a newly created Robot Build to the Hangar.
  • Fixed a bug where the spawn animations would occasionally not play correctly.
  • Fixed a bug where wrong values were shown for ‘Sprint Reactor’. The values now correctly show the speed in km/h.
