As a Wild Ten Pilot, it’s good to get to grips with your partner in battle—the War Robot. With several War Robots to choose from, each with its own unique attributes, picking the best ally for each fight is crucial.

Engineered around a classic combat frame inspired by early War Robots, the Relic Bulgasari is built for high-intensity frontline operations. Utilizing an asymmetric design with a signature heavy shoulder shield, it alters frontline dynamics by deploying a highly concentrated, linear strike via its Arpeggio ability, while its updated hardpoint configuration provides more tactical flexibility for diverse weapon loadouts.

Starting June 23, Relic Bulgasari will be available through Daily Deals, direct offers and the new Event Pass.

Combat Analysis

  • War Robot Model: Relic Bulgasari
  • Class: Assault
  • Dominion: Mayflower

Relic Bulgasari is a rugged, heavy-duty brawler that adapts the aesthetic of mobile classics with modern, cutting-edge engineering. Unlike its standard counterpart, this Assault variant is optimized for sustained mid-range shootouts and asymmetric tactical play.

Its defining feature is a massive, reinforced shoulder shield that can absorb staggering amounts of damage, allowing clever Pilots to tank incoming fire simply by angling their torso. Furthermore, the torso features a modification: a newly added light weapon slot for extra utility, and a re-engineered ability that focuses its rocket barrage into a tight, linear strike instead of a wide area splash, punishing enemies caught out of cover.

Consider using it with the new Relic Orkan, a rocket launcher built for the Assault class. Optimized for heavier setups, this variant trades burst fire for sustained pressure, featuring faster reload times and higher projectile velocity. The Relic Orkan shifts its power toward direct impact damage, rewarding high precision and direct hits to specific armor zones during prolonged clashes.
