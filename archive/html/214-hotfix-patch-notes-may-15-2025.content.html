Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on May 15, 2025.

Balancing Adjustments

Pilot Talents

  • The Pilot Talent “Bio Repair” has been adjusted:
    • Increased the value of Armor repaired from 2,000 per second to 4,000 per second.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug where the left Purifier Shoulder Module had customization options applied to it by default.
  • Fixed a bug where War Robot icons in the Workshop would sometimes load slowly or not at all.
  • Fixed a bug that allowed Cyclops’ Core Gear “Death Ray” to deal excessive damage when hitting an enemy from certain angles.
  • Fixed a bug where the currency icons for Battle Token to Credit conversion on the Battle Pass ending screen occasionally did not display.
  • Fixed a bug where players were able to receive double rewards by completing a Job under specific conditions.
