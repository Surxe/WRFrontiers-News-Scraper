The Battle for Tortuga is far from over. Amid orbital strikes, FortEvo agents carried out one of the most audacious operations in recent history. Through espionage, covert spies, and a rapidly escalating arms race, FortEvo succeeded in stealing fragments of Volta’s original Titan blueprint.

What emerged from FortEvo’s foundries was not a flawless replica, but a ruthless reinterpretation.

They named it Norna.

Norna is now available through Daily Deals and direct offers. She will also be available later via the Battle Pass and, after that, through general progression.

Combat Analysis

  • Titan Model: Norna
  • Class: Assault
  • Dominion: FortEvo

Designed for direct confrontation, Norna excels at breaking enemy lines and forcing engagements. Built from stolen intelligence and rushed innovation, it favors overwhelming force, area damage, and battlefield control.

Under the Hood

Make the most of your Titan by mastering its gear and weapons.

  • Coriolis - Heavy rocket launcher. Each rocker deals high damage and also damages nearby armor zones on hit.
  • Ion Disruptor - Release a large, slow-moving sphere that deals 5,800 damage per second to each armor zone it touches and disables enemy gear activation for 12.5s. Cooldown 30s.
  • Kinetic Barrier - Fires a force barrier in the aimed direction. The barrier blocks enemy projectiles and pushes back enemies on contact. It remains active until it takes 200,000 damage or until 12s have passed. Cooldown 34s.
  • Ion Volley - Continuously releases a fan of lightning orbs ahead for 8s. Each orb deals 8,500 damage on hit. Cooldown 30s.

The stats provided are for a maxed-out level.
