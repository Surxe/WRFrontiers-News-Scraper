As a Wild Ten Pilot, it’s good to get to grips with your partner in battle—the War Robot. With several War Robots to choose from, each with its own unique attributes, picking the best ally for each fight is crucial.

Defend to the end with Mesa, a War Robot that protects and buffs its allies while reducing the power of its enemies.

COMBAT ANALYSIS

  • War Robot Model: Mesa
  • Class: Defender
  • Dominion: FortEvo

The Mesa unit used to belong to a FortEvo enforcer who ended up missing in action, but Mesa somehow landed in The Lace's Chop Shop in near pristine condition…
Just like all current-gen FortEvo War Robots, Mesa is wired with complex biotech and can't be operated by non-augmented Pilots. However, Tortuga's mechanics managed to reverse engineer it and bring a new item to Tortuga's bustling black market.

UNDER THE HOOD

Make the most of your War Robot by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, Supply Gear, and Cycle Gear.

Don’t forget, you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite elements!

  • Harmonizer (Core Gear): Restores shield for nearby allies and half for Mesa itself. It also reduces damage output for nearby enemies.
  • Locust (Light Weapon - Kinetic): This mid-range shotgun is loaded with corrosive shells that deal extra damage over time.
  • Gamma Beam (Cycle Gear): Locks onto an enemy, dealing damage per second that increases based on proximity.
  • Ammo Fabricator (Supply Gear): Deploys a device that reduces the reload time of allies within range.
