Progress through Shipments to unlock powerful Titan, gear, and visual customizations inspired by stolen schematics and orbital conflict.

Orbital Nexus Battle Pass

The Orbital Nexus Battle Pass features the new assault Titan—Norna—and many other valuable rewards, including Salvage, Credits, Intel, cosmetics, and battle gear!

Battle Passes are only available for a limited time during Seasons. New Shipments of rewards will become available while the Battle Pass is active. You need to complete the Battle Pass while it’s available to unlock everything it has to offer!

  • Battle Pass (Free): Progress without spending a penny! Battle Tokens can be earned through battles and used to level up your Pass. You won’t be able to unlock every reward from all Shipments unless you upgrade to Battle Pass Gold.
  • Battle Pass Gold (200 WarpReals): Upgrade to Battle Pass Gold to earn enough Battle Tokens to obtain every item from the Battle Pass.

Upcoming Changes

Battle Token Conversion Change

Starting in Season 4, all unspent Battle Tokens at the end of a season will be automatically converted to Intel instead of Credits. Conversion rate:

  • 1 Battle Token = 6 Intel

Be sure to plan your progression accordingly as you move through Orbital Nexus and beyond.

Battle Pass Gold for Platinum Pioneer Pack Holders
