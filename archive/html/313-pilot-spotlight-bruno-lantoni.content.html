Background Analysis

  • Name: Bruno Lantoni
  • Class: Flanker
  • Dominion: Tortuga

Bruno Lantoni was once the undisputed icon of Grav Racing. Three-time champion. Corporate darling. A living logo stamped across arenas and sponsorship deals.

Then came the fractions of a millisecond.

To the public, nothing had changed. To Bruno, everything had. Reflexes dulled—barely measurable, but unacceptable. He did not slow down. He upgraded.

Bruno liquidated his fortune for experimental cybernetic augmentation. Muscle replaced with servos. Bone with alloy. Nerve with fiber-optic precision. The result: a fully augmented combat chassis wrapped in a sleek, racer-styled armored suit. Bright. Vivid. Built for speed, not ceremony.

The athletic commission banned him for life. “Excessive modification”.

So Bruno went where there are no rules—Tortuga. The Wild Ten. A new arena.

Now, on the anniversary of the conflict, he steps into robot warfare with the same objective he always had: win. Prove he is not obsolete. Prove he is not just machinery. Prove he is still the greatest athlete in the arena.

Tactical Talents

  • Feed Hacker: Your weapons reload quicker.

Rank 5: Ace Talent

  • Fragments Synergy: Every assist you make increases the primary and secondary parameter of your signature ability.

Bruno Lantoni doesn’t relax. He recalibrates.

The arena changed. The rules changed. His body changed.

The goal did not.
