Attention, Pilots! The following patch will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on December 23, 2025.

New Content

New Titan: Norna

The new Spider Titan, Norna, has been added, along with the Coriolis weapon.

New Common Pilot: Rex Cole

The new Common Pilot, Rex Cole, has been added to celebrate Christmas.

New Animated Wrap

The new animated wrap, Jolly Juggernaut, has been added for Christmas.

New Events

Cryo Gift Calendar (December 23-29)

Complete one battle per day to unwrap up to seven festive rewards, revealed day by day.

Intel Discount Event (December 23-29)

This week, the Tortuga engineers invite Pilots to take a closer look at the following builds:

  • War Robot Modules: Tyr, Fury, Scorpion, Grim (Titan)
  • Weapons: Vortex, Pulsar, Tusk, Nucleon, Katzbalger (Grim Titan)
  • Gear: Ghost Turret, Repulsor, Blink, Homing Missile, Gamma Beam

Solidus Node: Foundry is Back (December 23 – January 6)

Solidus Node: Foundry will return on December 23 and remain available in a dedicated unranked queue for two weeks.

Gameplay Adjustments

Player Progression

Player account progression has been expanded. The maximum player level has been increased to 200.

  • Anansi Shoulder Modules can now be crafted at level 173.
  • Anansi Chassis Modules can now be crafted at level 176.
  • Suppressor Supply Gear can now be crafted at level 179.
  • MLX2 Weapon can now be crafted at level 182.
  • Anansi Torso Modules can now be crafted at level 185.
  • Kumo Shoulder Modules can now be crafted at level 188.
  • Kumo Chassis Modules can now be crafted at level 191.
  • Horde Weapon can now be crafted at level 197.
  • Kumo Torso Modules can now be crafted at level 200.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the Mass Fuel Power-Up did not provide the correct restoration speed in the Last Robot Standing game mode.
  • Fixed an issue with the Grappling animation, which now activates correctly.
  • Fixed an issue with the Battle Pass background resolution.
