Designed for sustained engagements, Hitcher thrives at medium range, trading fire efficiently and applying steady pressure where it matters most.

Combat Analysis

  • War Robot Model: Hitcher
  • Class: Assault
  • Dominion: Freecon

Hitcher is a medium assault War Robot engineered for frontline stability. Its armor allows it to confidently engage enemies in one-on-one firefights, while its mobility ensures it can reposition when situations grow dangerous. Though not built to dive into tightly packed enemy groups, Hitcher excels at holding territory, supporting allies, and punishing exposed targets from mid-range.

Its combat philosophy is simple: endure, adapt, and outgun.

Under the Hood

Make the most of your War Robot by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, Supply Gear, and Cycle Gear.

Don’t forget, you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite elements!

  • Winged drone (Core Gear): Spawns a drone with flat 2,500 long wings that makes 14,600 damage to all the enemies on its way.
  • Nucleon (Electromagnetic, Autocannon, Heavy): Heavy plasma autocannon designed for sustained fire at medium range.
  • Nanite Repair (Cycle Gear): Repairs all modules for 3% armor per tick. Lasts 10 seconds.
  • Emergency Shield (Supply Gear): Restores 6,000 shield per second for 6 seconds.

The stats provided are for a maxed-out level.
