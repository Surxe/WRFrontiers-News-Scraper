Attention, Pilots! The following patch will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on September 30, 2025.

  • The new Intel Discount Event has launched (September 30 – October 7).
    • Ares, Anansi, Lancelot and Alpha, along with their gear and weapons, now have reduced Intel upgrade costs.
  • Solidus Node: Foundry has been added back to the map roster for all game modes via a separate unranked queue.
  • Bronze and Silver League players can now be matched with each other in matchmaking.
