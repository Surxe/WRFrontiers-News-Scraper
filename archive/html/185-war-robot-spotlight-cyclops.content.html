As a Wild Ten Pilot, it’s good to get to grips with your partner in battle—the War Robot. With 18 War Robots to choose from, each with their own unique attributes, picking the best ally for each fight is crucial.

Meet Cyclops, a powerful War Robot with a lethal ability that leaves destruction in its wake.

COMBAT ANALYSIS

  • War Robot Model: Cyclops
  • Class: Assault
  • Dominion: Mayflower

This Assault Class War Robot was once deemed an overpowered failure due to the immense collateral damage it caused, and thousands of these units were retired and put into long-term storage. However, the trade embargo in Tortuga has brought them back into service.

UNDER THE HOOD

Make the most of your Cyclops by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, Supply Gear, and Cycle Gear.

Don’t forget—you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite elements! 

  • Death Ray (Core Gear): Soup up this wide energy beam that deals intense damage It is so powerful that Cyclops can't move while it's active.
  • Gemini (Heavy Weapon - Rocket Launcher): A two-barrelled rocket launcher that fires heavy rockets in rapid succession.
  • Homing Missile (Cycle Gear): Fires a homing missile that deals damage to shields or armor.
  • Blink (Supply Gear): Teleport over a short distance in a direction you're facing.
