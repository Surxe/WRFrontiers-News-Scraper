Check out how to participate and the FAQs below.

How To Participate

  • Join the Discord and keep an eye on social channels for the next challenge!

Prizes

Each week includes:

  • 1 In-Game or Puzzle Challenge
    • Reward: 3,000 Credits
  • 1 Social Share Challenge
    • Reward: 70 Intel
  • Bonus: Complete both challenges in any one week to receive the Spark of Ingenuity Discord Role

FAQ

Q: How can I confirm my in-game name?
A: Check the guide here, you will find your in-game name above your User ID on your account page. 

Q: Can I share my Pilot Recruitment link in my social post?
A: Yes! Just make sure you meet the other challenge conditions too.

Q: Where do I submit my challenge entries?
A: Submit in the corresponding Discord channel each week, and include your in-game name.

Q: Do I need to complete both challenges to get rewards?
A: No, each completed challenge earns its own reward. Complete both in the same week for the Power Surge Discord Role.

Q: Can I use TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube for social challenges?
A: Yes, any social platform works. Just make sure to use #WRFrontiers and ensure your post is publicly viewable on the platform.

Q: Do I need to be good at editing or video making to enter?
A: Nope! Creativity and enthusiasm are what count most.
