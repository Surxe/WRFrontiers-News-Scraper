As a Wild Ten Pilot, it’s good to get to grips with your partner in battle—the War Robot. With several War Robots to choose from, each with its own unique attributes, picking the best ally for each fight is crucial.

Based on one of Mayflower's earliest combat frames, the Relic Lancelot is a legendary defender reborn for modern high-intensity warfare. This Relic variant undergoes a massive structural overhaul, trading some of its traditional weapon flexibility for maximum frontline durability. Equipped with enhanced protective systems and a supportive combat ability, it is designed to breach enemy lines while keeping its squad alive.

Starting July 7, Relic Lancelot will be available through Daily Deals, direct offers and the new Battle Pass.

Combat Analysis

  • War Robot Model: Relic Lancelot
  • Class: Defender
  • Dominion: Mayflower

To maximize frontline survivability, the standard model's light weapon slot has been replaced with reinforced shoulder armor plates on both sides. To compensate for this loss in utility, the torso was redesigned to accommodate a heavy weapon slot on top, allowing the robot to maintain a high damage profile during close-range engagements.

The main feature of this variant is the updated Paladin's Ward ability, which turns Lancelot into an aggressive brawler. Instead of just holding a defensive line, the robot can now push forward, taking massive damage while repairing nearby teammates at the same time. Paladin's Ward grants Lancelot 35% damage resistance for 10s. While active nearby allies restore armor equal to 45% of the damage absorbed by Lancelot. 

For the best results, pair the robot with the new Relic Thunder shotgun. Even though its increased weight requires a heavier setup, its low energy consumption and extra point-blank damage perfectly complement Lancelot’s aggressive, push-focused playstyle.
