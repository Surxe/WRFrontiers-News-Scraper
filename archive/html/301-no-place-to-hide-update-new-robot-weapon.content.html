New War Robot: Hitcher

Built for endurance and battlefield control, Hitcher is a War Robot engineered for sustained medium-range engagements. It thrives where firefights drag on, trading blows efficiently, anchoring lanes, and applying constant pressure until enemies crack.

Designed to hold ground and punish overextensions, Hitcher rewards disciplined positioning and steady aggression.

Available in Daily Deals and Direct Offers.

New Weapon: Slugger

Slugger is a light autocannon that delivers devastating damage up close, shredding targets at short range before rapidly losing effectiveness over distance. Perfect for ambushes, brawls, and aggressive flanking tactics, Slugger turns proximity into power.

When fights get personal, Slugger finishes them fast.

Available in Daily Deals and Direct Offers.
