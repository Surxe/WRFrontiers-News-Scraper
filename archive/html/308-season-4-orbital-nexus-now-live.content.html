Leagues & Ranked Updates

Season 4 brings smarter League placements and improved matchmaking designed to make ranked play more rewarding for everyone. League Point thresholds have been adjusted to reduce grind and help players climb more smoothly, while still preserving the thrill of earning your rank through consistent performance.

Ranked matchmaking has also been restructured to create fairer, more competitive battles. Bronze and Silver, Gold and Platinum, and Emerald through Noachium players are now matched within tighter skill ranges, ensuring every fight is balanced and exciting. Plus, the maximum queue time for larger crews has been cut in half, so teams spend less time waiting and more time battling.
