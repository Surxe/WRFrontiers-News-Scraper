As a Wild Ten Pilot, it’s good to get to grips with your partner in battle—the War Robot. With 18 War Robots to choose from, each with their own unique attributes, picking the best ally for each fight is crucial.

Lay down your sword for Lancelot, a gallant War Robot with defensive abilities fit for a true knight.

COMBAT ANALYSIS

  • War Robot Model: Lancelot
  • Class: Defender
  • Dominion: Mayflower

Defenders like Lancelot are designed to hold team positions, and this particular War Robot is perfect for Pilots who value durability above all else. Lancelot is as big as it is tough, and can regenerate when its allies take damage. 

Even though Lancelot can’t deal with flankers due to poor mobility, it can still prove its namesake’s chivalry by taking down large, slow enemies to protect its teammates. 

UNDER THE HOOD

Make the most of your War Robot by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, Supply Gear, and Cycle Gear.

Don’t forget—you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite elements! 

  • Battle Order (Core Gear): Allies close by take less damage. Whenever they get hit, you regenerate armor. 
  • Punisher (Light Weapon - Kinetic): Punisher lives up to its name with its large ammo pool and fast fire.
  • Reflector (Cycle Gear): Returns a percentage of incoming damage back at the enemy for a short period of time. 
  • Sprint Reactor (Supply Gear): Speeds you up for a short amount of time.
