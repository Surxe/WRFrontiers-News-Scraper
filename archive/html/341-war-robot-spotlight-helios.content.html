Combat Analysis

  • War Robot Model: Helios
  • Class: Flanker
  • Dominion: Mayflower

Helios is a high-speed flanker, perfect for Pilots who prefer to dictate the pace of engagement and disrupt enemy positions. While it trades vertical jump height for low-cost hovering, its exceptional speed and heavy hardpoint allow it to outmaneuver pursuers and punish high-value targets from unexpected angles. Helios utilized brand new type of chassis with two movement abilities:

Throttle Burst (Dash)
Instantly boosts speed and mobility, allowing the robot to navigate steep inclines, tackle challenging terrain, and use ramps as springboards.

Bounce Drive (Jump)
Unlike standard jetpacks, it trades raw launch altitude for low-cost hovering and sustained airtime.

Though Helios lacks defensive capabilities for sustained frontline brawling, it excels at fracturing enemy formations using its unprecedented mobility and tactical utility. By dropping pools of burning napalm with Flaming Rider to deter chasers and utilizing instant teleportation to evade danger, it ensures enemy positions remain unstable.

Consider using it with new heavy cannon: Pyre. A heavy flamethrower effective only at close range. Ideal for finishing off heavily damaged tanks or flanking backline robots. However, it struggles against enemies with sturdy shields and ability to quickly open up distance. Thanks to its AoE effect, the weapon is less aim-dependent, but far more demanding when it comes to maintaining optimal engagement range.
