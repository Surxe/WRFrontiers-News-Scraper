Pilots, thank you for your continued support and patience as we shape the future of War Robots: Frontiers.

Over the past few months, we've been carefully planning how new robots and weapons are introduced into the game. We know how important it is to maintain a fair, transparent, and enjoyable experience for all players, especially as the battlefield evolves with new content.

With that in mind, we want to take a moment to share how new content will be handled moving forward. Our goal is to keep things clear and consistent for every pilot, whether you're just starting out or deep into your journey.

So, here’s what’s changing, and how we plan to introduce new gear in a way that supports a healthy game economy and progression system.

How New Robots and Weapons Will Be Released 

Our goal is to give you early access opportunities while ensuring that new content gradually becomes accessible to players through regular gameplay. 

  • First, new robots and weapons will debut through limited-time direct sales (including Daily Deals). These offers will be available for a short period and are designed for players who want to get their hands on the latest tech right away.
  • Next, they’ll be featured in an upcoming Battle Pass or Event Pass. This gives players another chance to earn them through gameplay while also enjoying seasonal content and rewards.

What’s Coming Next

We know many of you are eager to plan ahead, so here’s a look at the upcoming content schedule and what to expect in the weeks ahead.

  • September 23–30 – The limited-time sale for the new Kumo War Robot is live! This is your first chance to deploy Kumo on the battlefield. But don’t worry if you miss it—Kumo will be making its way into the Battle Pass starting October 14.
  • October 14 – Season 3 begins, and with it comes a wave of new content. Throughout the season, we’ll be introducing new spider-type robots and weapons, each bringing unique abilities and playstyles to shake up the meta. We’ll share more details about each addition as they’re released, so stay tuned for regular updates.
  • Also launching on October 14, several powerful robots and weapons will be added to the general progression system. This includes the Mesa and Ceres War Robots, along with the Nucleon and Apollo weapons. If you haven’t had a chance to try them yet, you’ll soon be able to unlock them through standard progression.

There’s a lot on the horizon, and we’re excited to bring this new content to life as War Robots: Frontiers continues to evolve.

Stay tuned—you won’t want to miss what’s coming next!
