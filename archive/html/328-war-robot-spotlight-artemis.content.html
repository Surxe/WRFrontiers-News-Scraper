Combat Analysis

  • War Robot Model: Artemis
  • Class: Tactician
  • Dominion: Mayflower

Artemis is a nimble backline predator, perfect for Pilots who prefer to dictate the flow of battle while staying out of the crossfire. Though light on armor, its exceptional mobility and light hardpoints with long range weaponry allow it to punish enemies from the safety of the rear.

While Artemis lacks the raw durability for frontline brawling, it excels at dismantling high-value targets using Hunter’s Mark. By exposing enemies and weakening their plating, it ensures the vanguard stays healthy; every mark-assisted takedown repairs the armor of nearby allies, turning Artemis into a crucial lifeline for the team's heaviest hitters.
