Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on May 23, 2025.

General Adjustments

  • Launched the Intel Discount Event for the War Robots “Orochi” and “Phantom”.
    • Intel upgrade prices are significantly reduced for those War Robots.

Matchmaking

  • Players in Silver League will now match with players in Gold League.

Technical Improvements

  • Improved game performance.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug that caused the PlayStation 5 client to crash when using the weapon “Shocktrain”.
  • Fixed a bug where the pop-up videos in the Training Battles (Tutorial) were not muted.
  • Fixed a bug where the Store would automatically scroll all the way to the top.
  • Fixed two Premium-related bugs:
    • Fixed a bug where Premium Jobs were not locked after the Premium Account status expired.
    • Fixed occurrences of a bug where Premium Jobs were not unlocked after acquiring the Premium Account status.

Follow the game on X and Facebook and join the official Discord server to get all the latest hotfix info.
