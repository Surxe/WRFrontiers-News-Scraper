What goes up, must come down—and the same goes for the Warp network. The only thing connecting the Tortuga space station to the rest of the Wild Ten system has shut down, bringing almost all activities to a dead stop. 

As the battle for Terminus rages on in the background, and Pilots are only just recovering from a life of scavenging and building due to the ongoing blockade, this new problem poses the greatest risk of all to life on Tortuga. 

With no Warp Gates live, power is at a premium. Pilots now scramble to build their own Warp Probes to re-establish transport routes and keep the lights on. However, even a brief Warp blackout was enough to put the entire system out of whack. 

Ingenuity is the name of the game, and some Pilots have the resourcefulness and spark to keep life—and war—going on the Wild Ten.
