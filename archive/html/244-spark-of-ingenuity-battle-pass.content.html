Restoration of the Wild Ten warp network continues, and the resourceful Pilots happen upon a merchant vessel. With no manifest, records, or crew aboard, the cargo of supplies and Modules it carries is too tempting to pass up. The salvage operation begins...

The Spark of Ingenuity Battle Pass is your way of evolving and adapting to new battles on the Wild Ten. Play to earn XP and Battle Tokens, level up your Pass, and exchange your Battle Tokens for new electrifying content.

Spark of Ingenuity Battle Pass

The Spark of Ingenuity Battle Pass features the new defensive War Robot—Mesa—and many other valuable rewards, including Salvage, Credits, Premium Account, unique cosmetics, and battle gear!

Battle Passes are only available for a limited time during Seasons. New Shipments of rewards will become available while the Battle Pass is active. You need to complete the Battle Pass while it’s available to unlock everything it has to offer!

  • Battle Pass (Free): Progress without spending a penny! Battle Tokens can be earned through battles and used to level up your Pass. You won’t be able to unlock every reward from all Shipments unless you upgrade to Battle Pass Gold.
  • Battle Pass Gold (200 WarpReals): Upgrade to Battle Pass Gold to earn enough Battle Tokens to obtain every item from the Battle Pass.
