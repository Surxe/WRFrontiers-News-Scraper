The final chapter of Season 4 begins.

Freecon’s expansion into the Wild Ten is no longer a distant ambition—it is a reality unfolding across the battlefield. As their control tightens and resistance begins to fracture, Pilots must rise, adapt, and seize every advantage in a war where momentum shifts by the second.

The Mirror Threat Battle Pass brings experimental firepower, rare resources, and striking cosmetics shaped by this new era of conflict. Featuring the formidable Conflux and Skuld Titan weapons for Volta and Norna, this Pass equips you to confront the escalating threat head-on.

Mirror Threat Battle Pass (April 14 - May 18)

Progress through Shipments to unlock Kumo War Robot Modules, Horde, Conflux and Skuld Weapons, and a wide array of visual customizations shaped by the dangers and mysteries of the Wild Ten.

Battle Passes are active only during their Season, and new Shipments unlock as the Pass progresses. Complete the Battle Pass before May 18 to claim every reward.

  • Battle Pass (Free): Progress without spending a penny! Battle Tokens can be earned through battles and used to level up your Pass. You won’t be able to unlock every reward from all Shipments unless you upgrade to Battle Pass Gold.
  • Battle Pass Gold (200 WarpReals): Upgrade to Battle Pass Gold to earn enough Battle Tokens to obtain every item from the Battle Pass.
