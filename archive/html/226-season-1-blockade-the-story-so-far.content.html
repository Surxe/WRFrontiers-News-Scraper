MERCENARY CONTRACTS REPORTED ON THE WILD TEN

By Duke Blanko, Reporter (Mars News Network)

New reports from the Wild Ten today revealed that mercenary contracts have been issued to War Robot Pilots by the Freecon, Mayflower, and FortEvo Dominions. Military strategists and politicians have been meeting in secret to discuss the impact this will have on much-needed trade and mining facilities across the Wild Ten, once considered a safe haven for trade. 

War Robots are said to be the ultimate war machines, equipped with top-of-the-line weapons and gear, and are only surpassed by a new technology—Titans. Even bigger and more dangerous, a Titan can wipe out a whole team of War Robots, leading to even more battles, and even more contracts for eager Pilots to take on. 

As the residents, mechanics, leaders, and Pilots begin their journey down the road of war, only time will tell if these upcoming battles will result in anything other than mass destruction.

CHAOS ERUPTS AS TERMINUS SUPPLY LINES CUT

By Duke Blanko, Reporter (Mars News Network)

Chaos erupted today at Terminus, the gateway to the Wild Ten system. Pilots and residents alike could be seen panicking as supply lines were blocked, leaving the black market open for a comeback. One resourceful Pilot who refused to give his name (despite appearing to be followed by a lie detector drone) described the recent events as “an opportunity to make some quick cash with some bits I found… lying around.” 

Mechanics are especially concerned with the supply port cut off, but have vowed to rebuild War Robots for Pilots taking on contracts. One such mechanic named “The Lace” told us, “The black market may be sketchy, but these Pilots need Modules, and we will provide them one way or another.” 

While some Pilots fear an escalating conflict, for most, it's just another day in the Wild Ten.
