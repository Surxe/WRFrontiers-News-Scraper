These are the patch notes for the build available on December 8. Update your game to the latest version and play War Robots: Frontiers today!

Match formation rules have been changed:

  • If there are 12 players in the queue, then the match starts immediately
  • If there are 6 or more players in the queue, then the match starts no later than 30 seconds
  • If there are 2 or more players in the queue, then the match starts no later than 60 seconds
  • If there is 1 player in the queue, then the match starts in 90 seconds
