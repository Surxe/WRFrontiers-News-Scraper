Starting with the Elimination Update, you can join a Squad with your friends for a whole new dimension of competitive play in War Robots: Frontiers!

Simply create a Squad, then copy and share your Squad Code (or enter another player’s Squad Code) to assemble teams of up to six players. When you’re ready, you can head into battle against other Squads and players.

If you’re struggling to find a Squad, you can find fellow Pilots on Discord. Share your Squad Code or put out a request in the Squads channel to get started!
