Pilots, it’s your time to make your mark on the Wild Ten! We’re launching a community event to create a brand-new in-game cosmetic Sticker for War Robots: Frontiers until August 24.

You can submit your design via Instagram or X by tagging @WRFrontiers and using #WRFStickers, or you can submit it to our dedicated sticker-creation-contest Discord channel.

Share your Sticker before 23:59 CEST on August 24 for the chanceto be one of the lucky winners who will receive:

  • Top Prize: Their Sticker reworked by our team and added to the game
  • Top 3: 300 WarpReals

Check out the rules below (and the General Contest Rules), and good luck! 

Rules
  • Your entry must be an original game-themed cosmetic Sticker (in PNG or JPG format) inspired by War Robots: Frontiers
  • Ideally, your image should be designed to fit well within a square 1:1 ratio format.
  • You may suggest a name and description for your sticker, as you would like it to be displayed in-game. Note that the company reserves the right to modify these to better fit War Robots: Frontiers and its universe.
  • Your entry cannot feature any pre-existing, copyrighted, or third-party material. Only entries featuring original artwork inspired by War Robots: Frontiers will be considered.
  • Images should be clearly discernible: Do not include any text, signatures, or other elements that could obscure the image, nor any border elements around it.
  • Avoid any offensive imagery within your submission. Specifically, no blood, skeletons, dead bodies, racist, sexist, political, or religious iconography (respectfully used or otherwise).
  • Entries must include at least one work-in-progress screenshot/picture to show the creation process.
  • Only one of your entries can win, but you can submit multiple entries. This is applicable for multiple versions of the same content across various regions.
  • Entrants found to violate the Terms of Service or break competition rules are subject to disqualification up to and beyond the conclusion of the competition.
  • Competition judges reserve the right to disqualify an entry without recourse or explanation - in such instances, we will seek to justify our decision in private if it is appropriate to do so.
  • The best entries will be determined by MY.GAMES War Robots: Frontiers team and winners will be announced on the War Robots: Frontiers social media after the contest has ended.
  • You agree that your entry can be added to the game client of War Robots: Frontiers.
  • You agree that your entry can be used by MY.GAMES for promotional purposes (for example, for publication on our official social channels or websites) across all territories in perpetuity.
  • You agree that your entry can be altered by MY.GAMES for use within the game client of War Robots: Frontiers.
  • The winning entry will only be added to the game at the discretion of MY.GAMES.
