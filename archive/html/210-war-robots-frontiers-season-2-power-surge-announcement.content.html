Scavenger’s Haul Battle Pass

Boost your Scavenger’s Haul Battle Pass (arriving June 17) to unlock dozens of adrenaline-pumping rewards, including exclusive cosmetics, battle gear, and a new War Robot: Loki!

New Titan: Volta 

Turn up the voltage with a brand new Titan: Volta. This live wire Titan is the first quadruped to be built on Tortuga, and it’s as aggressive as it is electrifying. With its ability to double jump and hover, these new traversal capabilities are sure to set Volta apart from other Titans on the battlefield. 

Activate its electric weapon, generate a moving barrier, or block projectiles and deal damage to enemies foolish enough to pass through.

Map Updates: Spectrum & Fissure

Much has happened in the Wild Ten since the beginning of the blockade. Freecon rebuilt the Spectrum Research Facility, seemingly preparing for the next stage of their mysterious experiment, and the Fissure mining site was taken over by Warp anomalies. Despite that, mining operations here are still ongoing. 

Have any of these events caused the Warp blackout? At this point, we can only speculate.

What we know for sure is that both the Spectrum and Fissure maps received a massive facelift, along with better weather setups and improved terrain readability.

New Game Mode: Spearhead

Powerslide into a brand-new game mode: Spearhead. 

Two teams fight over randomly spawning Warp Probes to stabilize Warp in the region. Locate and capture them first to earn Points! Hit the Points cap, stay ahead, or eliminate all enemies to win. 

New War Robots: Loki & Mesa 

Loki

Trickster by name, trickster by nature. Loki's ability to use holographic projections makes it an excellent infiltrator and scout. Its armor is strong for its class, and its anti-shield firepower means it can easily take on flankers, though it should avoid encounters with heavier foes. Add Loki to your Drop Team and see what this stealthy War Robot can do. 

Unlock Loki through the Scavenger’s Haul Battle Pass (coming June 17).

Mesa

Defend ‘til the end with Mesa, a strong defender with amazing ally-protecting abilities. Deploy barriers to shield Mesa and its teammates from projectiles, or unleash a biotech drone that follows alongside it, buffing allies and weakening enemies in the area. 

Make Mesa your mate later in Season 2 through the Spark Of Ingenuity Battle Pass (coming July 22). 

New Hero Pilot: Sora Sing

Unleash the sparky Sora Sing as your War Robot’s new Pilot. This infamous femme fatale has found herself with a public persona she may not deserve, taking the fall for war crimes which saw her stripped of her citizenship, and expatriated to the Wild Ten—the only place that would take her. 

While her fellow Pilots may not trust her just yet, her talents hit all the high notes, and her ability to reload faster and boost damage (among others) make her a more than capable Pilot. Remember, it’s not over until the Sora Sings…

Sora Sing will be available in the Store.

Updated Leagues Rewards System

In a recent Dev Diary, we announced that rewards from previous League ranks would also be earnable. For example, if you reach Gold in Milestone 1, you will receive Bronze, Silver, and Gold rewards. However, if you reach Diamond in Milestone 2, you will receive only the Milestone 2 rewards for Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, and Diamond.

We are not changing the way that Milestone rewards work in Season 2, meaning that if a player joins during Milestone 2, they cannot obtain the Milestone 1 rewards 

The total number of leagues and requirement points will stay the same, but players will drop Leagues at the beginning of Season 2. For example, if you land in Silver at the end of Season 1, you will begin in Bronze in Season 2.
