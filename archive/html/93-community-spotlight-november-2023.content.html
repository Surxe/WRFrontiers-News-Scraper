Development Insights

We regularly share some of the concepts and tweaks we are working on via our Discord to give 

players a sneak peek into the development process. Here are a few of the things we shared in November:

Improved Robot Animations

We are continually improving our Robots’ walking, running, and landing animations. When accelerating and braking, Robots lean forward or backward, and when turning, they can tilt accordingly.

Ravana Wallpaper

We couldn't help but share this stunning art featuring Ravana. Feel free to use it for your content or wallpapers!

Introducing the Updated Mont

Before the Autumn update, we shared a walkaround of the new Mont Map, showing off the main changes, its icy landscape, increased destructibility, and an overall improved sense of scale.

Robot Colour Schemes

We noticed a few comments on Ravana’s color scheme and wanted to share our take on the original. Let us know if you would like to see more unique color schemes on future Robots or if you’d prefer we retain more of the original design.
