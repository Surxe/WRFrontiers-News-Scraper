Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on December 5, 2024. 

General Fixes & Improvements

  • Descriptions for the following abilities have been adjusted to include Area of Effect (AoE) Damage:
    • Steel Feathers
    • Cold Snap
    • Blast Wave
    • Iron Rain
  • The ability “Echo Burst” has been rebalanced:
    • Now deals equal damage to shields throughout the whole blast radius, but damage to shields in every armor zone has been reduced by 50%. At level 13, the damage has been reduced from 16,000 to 8,000.
  • The reload behavior of the weapon “Gozer” has been changed.
    • The weapon no longer continuously reloads while shooting. It is now a magazine.
    • The reload speed is 2.5 seconds (across all levels).
    • Damage to shields increases with upgrade level. At level 13, the damage to shields has been increased from 322 to 350.
  • The calculation formula for ATK/DEF/MOB (Attack, Defense, Mobility) has been reworked.
    • These stats should now reflect the strengths of weapons and abilities more accurately.
  • The charge accumulation rates for Cycle Gear and Titan Charge for all weapons.
    • It now takes roughly 3 minutes of continuous shooting to charge a Titan and 1 minute to charge up 250 units of charge.
