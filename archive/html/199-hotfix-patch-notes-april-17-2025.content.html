Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on April 17, 2025.

Matchmaking

  • Players in Gold League will only match with other Gold players, and no longer match with Platinum and above.

General Adjustments

  • Prepared the upcoming Battle Pass: Smuggler’s Run, which launches on April 22, 2025.
  • Adjusted the level requirement to show the Shop Banner which includes information to where to obtain Robots and Robot Modules for players at Level 10 and below.

Gameplay Adjustments

  • Added a new Daily Job, in addition to the existing three Jobs (five for Premium Account holders). This new Daily Job will always have the same completion conditions: Win 1 Battle.
  • Updated a job description to include that a specific Torso is needed to complete the job for the following:
    • Capture 6 Warp Gates using Orochi or Harpy Torso.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug where weapon hit registration was inconsistent.
  • Returned the missing description for Typhon’s Core Gear: Blackout.
  • Fixed a bug where the Bulwark’s Core Gear “Absorber Sphere” would treat shots from “Thunder” weapons as one projectile. “Absorber Sphere” now correctly recognizes each pellet as a projectile and absorbs them as intended.
  • Fixed a bug where the rarity of Warp “Infinity Breaker” was incorrect.
  • Fixed a bug where the weapon “Shocktrain” would deal 200% of the intended damage when the game was set to 30 FPS.
  • Fixed a bug where Pilot Talent trigger notifications for proximity based Pilot Talents would repeat endlessly.
  • Fixed a bug where players were able to increase the aiming height when aiming down sights with specific weapons, which allowed them to aim over walls and cover.
  • Fixed a bug on the map “Fissure” where the camera would clip through certain parts of terrain and structures.
  • Fixed a bug where purchases on PlayStation could timeout which would result in a “Failed to get purchase” error.
