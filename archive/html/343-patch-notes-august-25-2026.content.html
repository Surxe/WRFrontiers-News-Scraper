Leagues

With the start of Season 6 league placements will be reset, and ranks adjusted. League Points will be assigned based on your new League placement:

  • Bronze: 0 League Points (bottom of Bronze League)
  • Silver: 300 League Points (bottom of Silver League)
  • Gold & Platinum: 500 League Points (bottom of Gold League)
  • Emerald, Diamond & Noachium: 1500 League Points (bottom of Emerald League)

New Battle Pass: Tooth and Claw (August 25 – September 14)

Unlock two Relic robots: the latest Relic Scorpion and Relic Bulgasari as well as new Pinata weapons, Relic Orkans, various resources and cosmetics!

New War Robot: Relic Scorpion

A high-mobility FortEvo Flanker built for strategic map control. Its long-range teleportation is ideal for capturing beacons, ambushing backline artillery, or executing retreating targets. Lacking heavy armor, it relies on calculated strikes rather than diving into crowd fights.

Starting August 25, Relic Scorpion will be available through Daily Deals, direct offers and the new Battle pass.

New Weapon: Pinata

Lightweight burst-fire rocket launcher for close-range fights. Simple to use—just get close and unload. Fires its entire magazine in one burst with no ammo management, making it an ideal secondary or an option for multi-weapon builds.

Starting August 25, Pinata will be available through Daily Deals, direct offers and the new Battle pass.

New Weapon: Relic Pinata

Re-engineered Pinata. This variant shares Relic Orkan's precise firing profile. It offers consistent DPS and controlled rocket bursts, trading ease of use for superior fire management and sustained firepower.

Starting August 25, Relic will be available through Daily Deals and direct offers. It might be featured in a Battle Pass or Event Pass later.

Balance Changes

Conflux

  • Armor Damage increased from 2500 to 2850

Slugger

  • Shield Damage decreased from 1550 to 1150

Velite

  • Armor damage decreased from 4880 to 4400
  • Shield damage decreased from 6400 to 5500

Anansi Chassis

  • Armor decreased from 68100 to 63300

Crix Chassis

  • Armor decreased from 70490 to 65600

Fury Torso - Warp Reload

  • Cooldown decreased from 22s to 20s
  • Delay on levels 1 to 6 decreased from 0.8s to 0.5s

Harpy Torso - Napalm Shell

  • Damage decreased from 5000 to 4000

Tyr Torso - Repairing Drone

  • Duration decreased from 10s to 9s

Homing Missile Gear

  • Gear Recharge decreased from 350 to 300

Minefield Gear

  • Damage decreased from 9000 to 6800

Reflector Gear

  • Duration increased from 10s to 12s

Chemical Hazard Talent

  • Damage boost decreased from 15% to 10%
  • Duration boost decreased from 15% to 12%

Bugfixes and various changes

  • Added animations for the Helios torso ability.

  • Fixed an issue where the Helios chassis would rotate automatically while flying.

  • Fixed a bug where hits from Helios's ability failed to trigger the Press The Attack and Maximizer talents.

  • Added ability cooldown scaling to the Tesla Coil tooltip based on Volta shoulder upgrades.

  • Adjusted the Gear Hacker talent to apply damage stat increases more accurately across various abilities.

  • Fixed an exploit on the Fissure map to prevent shoot-throughs.
