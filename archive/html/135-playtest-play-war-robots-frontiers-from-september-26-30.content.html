Ready to take on the Wild Ten? Play War Robots: Frontiers as part of our Playtest from October 3–7 to get a sneak peek at upcoming new content including the Battle Pass, test your metal with new Robots and Pilots, and help shape the future of mercenary mecha mayhem. 

IMPORTANT: This Playtest is only available to Pioneer Pack owners. It allows us to test the stability of new builds with a clean slate before deploying to the main Early Access version of War Robots: Frontiers. We are also considering launching a Public Test Server to let you playtest even more upcoming content in the future.

Playtest Dates & Times

  • Start: Thursday, October 3 at 16:00 CEST (15:00 BST / 07:00 PDT / 10:00 EDT)
  • End: Monday, October 7 at 10:00 CEST (09:00 BST / 01:00 PDT / 04:00 EDT)

New Content

  • Battle Pass: Put the pedal to the metal to earn Legendary Pilots, new Weapons and Gear, and new Robots. 
  • New Robots: Tear up the battlefield with Scorpion and Pursuer. 
  • New Pilots: Clock in as new Legendary Pilots Markus Shedd and Ever, along with a redesigned Kate “Sparrow” Sinclair. 
  • New Weapons: Blast ‘em into cosmic dust with Noricum, and welcome the return of Tusk.

IMPORTANT: Progress made in the Playtest will not be reflected in the Early Access version of the game.

Playtest Rewards

Participants will receive a “Playtest Crew” Sticker, 1 day of Premium, and 6,000 Credits for completing the in-game survey.

IMPORTANT: The Playtest Crew Sticker will remain available to you when the game leaves Early Access. Other rewards are subject to progress wipes.
