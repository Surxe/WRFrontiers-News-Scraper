New War Robot: Relic Bulgasari

A legendary classic reborn. Relic Bulgasari fights the frontline with an asymmetric shoulder shield, an extra light weapon slot, and a precise linear strike via its modified Arpeggio ability.

Starting June 23, Relic Bulgasari will be available through Daily Deals, direct offers and the new Event pass.

New Robot Weapon: Relic Orkan

Rocket launcher built for the Assault class. Optimized for heavier setups, it features faster reload times, higher projectile velocity, and a high-precision direct damage profile for sustained firefights.

Starting June 23, Relic Orkan will be available through Daily Deals and direct offers.
