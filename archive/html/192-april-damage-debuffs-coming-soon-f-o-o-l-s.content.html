Pilots on the Wild Ten have lots to contend with—gunfire, explosions, and even environmental damage, but we think you deserve even more mayhem. Have you ever been in the middle of fighting for a Warp Gate and started to get blurry vision or even get a warning to plug in your mech? No? Well, now you will! 

Introducing Pilot & War Robot Damage Debuffs, coming June 31!

This new feature, known as Faulty Operational Overload Logic System (F.O.O.L.S), will add debuffs to your Pilot and War Robot, making battles even more realistic the more damage you take! 

Vertigo

Battling in a War Robot can take its toll on even the hardiest of Pilots. Fight to stay in control with the new Vertigo debuff as it becomes harder and harder to stay on course!

Concussion 

Shoot, dodge, jump! Wait…where am I? The new Concussion debuff adds all the fun of being utterly confused to the battlefield! 

Low Battery

Did you forget to plug in your War Robot before bed again? The new Low Battery debuff adds technological realism to your battles, and reminds you to always leave the house with a spare portable charger.

Damage Assessment

Diagnostics never rest! The new Damage Assessment effect will help you to better understand the ins and outs of how your War Robot works. It takes a while though…

Overheat

Staying cool in the heat of battle sometimes simply isn’t possible. Feel the heat with the new Overheat effect, taking you to new highs (temperatures, that is).
