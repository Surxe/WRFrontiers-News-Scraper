As factions clash among storm-worn ridges and fortified ports, Carmen Ruiz steps forward in a heavily armored Decker War Robot, equipped with a powerful weapon and reinforced modules. Her mission is to lead her Dominion to a hard-earned victory

The Damage Resistance Battle Pass provides every Pilot with the tools they need to survive the chaos. Earn XP and Battle Tokens, level up your Pass, and unlock shipment after shipment of defensive gear, Martian-themed cosmetics, powerful weapons, resources, and the new Hero Pilot: Carmen Ruiz herself.

Damage Resistance Battle Pass

This Battle Pass features a wealth of tactical upgrades—including multiple Hefty Weapons, Decker modules, Supply and Cycle Gear, Paints, Cosmetics, Wear & Tear, planet-inspired Stickers, and the long-awaited Carmen Ruiz Hero Pilot.

Battle Passes are active only during their Season, and new Shipments unlock as the Pass progresses. Complete the Battle Pass before January 18 to claim every reward.

  • Battle Pass (Free): Progress without spending a penny! Battle Tokens can be earned through battles and used to level up your Pass. You won’t be able to unlock every reward from all Shipments unless you upgrade to Battle Pass Gold.
  • Battle Pass Gold (200 WarpReals): Upgrade to Battle Pass Gold to earn enough Battle Tokens to obtain every item from the Battle Pass.
