Pilot your very own god of war with Ares, a War Robot with flanking abilities that allow it to take the fight to the enemy and return to its allies to regroup.

COMBAT ANALYSIS

  • War Robot Model: Ares
  • Class: Flanker
  • Dominion: Mayflower

Just like the god of war himself, Ares thrives on conflict, and is ready to take on any and all enemies on the battlefields of the Wild Ten.

Ares is a flanker, designed for quick attacks against isolated targets. Despite its lightweight construction, it can inflict tremendous damage up close, and its Core Gear (Wrath) allows Ares to push forward or safely retreat when it becomes vulnerable.

UNDER THE HOOD

Make the most of your War Robot by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, Supply Gear, and Cycle Gear.

Don’t forget—you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite elements!

  • Wrath (Core Gear): Activate a built-in barrier and extra weapons.
  • Scourge (Light Weapon - Electromagnetic): Sustains a focused close-range electric beam.
  • Flashbang (Cycle Gear): Fire a homing projectile that deals damage and blinds the target for a short period.
  • Holofield (Supply Gear): Deploy a wall that blocks line of sight.
