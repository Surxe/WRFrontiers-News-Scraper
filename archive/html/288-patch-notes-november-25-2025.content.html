Attention, Pilots! The following patch will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on November 25, 2025.

New Content

Un-Cover Tortuga Event Pass (November 25 – December 8)

Whether you’re on the Free track or looking to upgrade your Battle Pass for additional rewards, a wide range of items is available to be unlocked.

  • Zenit (Heavy Rocket Launcher)
  • Kinetic Pulse (Supply Gear)
  • Sarah Ali (Common Pilot)
  • Emma James (Hero Pilot)
  • 4 Stickers, 1 Wrap
  • Intel, Credits & Salvage

New Events

Intel Discount Event (November 25 – December 2)

This week, the Tortuga engineers invite Pilots to take a closer look at the following Builds:

  • War Robot Modules: Raven, Bulwark, Ceres, Matriarch (Titan)
  • Weapons: Gozer, Thunder, Bisector, Hive (Matriarch)
  • Gear: Blast Wave, Infinite Ammo, Holofield, Nanite Repair, Ghost Turret, Reflector

Solidus Node: Foundry is Back (November 25 – December 9)

  • Solidus Node: Foundry will return on November 25 and remain available in a dedicated unranked queue for two weeks.

Gameplay Adjustments

Balance Changes

The following adjustments apply to modules at max level:

Zenit

  • Decreased reload time from 3s to 2.57s
  • Increased AOE radius from 22m to 24m

Fowler

  • Increased armor damage from 6,800 per shot to 7,100 per shot

Bisector

  • Decreased rate of fire from 60 rounds per minute to 0.87 shots per second

Holofield

  • Increased duration from 10s to 13s

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug causing Crix to appear above the platform in the Hangar
  • Fixed timing issues for the Gripping animations
