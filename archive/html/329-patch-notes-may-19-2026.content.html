Quadrupedal Chassis Adjustments

The concept of Leg Armor has been updated to Armor Zones. This new term more accurately describes complex chassis types, such as quadrupedal models, as well as future chassis designs.
Quadrupedal Chassis Adjustments: We’ve updated how armor values are displayed for four-legged chassis. While individual Armor Zone values are no longer doubled on the UI, the base armor for these zones has been increased to compensate for their larger hitboxes.
