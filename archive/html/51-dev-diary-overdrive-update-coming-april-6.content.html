The Overdrive Update arrives in War Robots: Frontiers on April 6, and it’s stuffed to the brim with new and exciting content. 

Level up your gear with Upgrades, and spend Alloys to instantly improve your equipment.

Pilot the brand-new Robot Siren and use its sniper and hover abilities to move tactically around the battlefield and set up for the perfect shot. 

Equip your loadout with two new weapons—Swarm and Sticky Gun. Sticky Gun shoots sticky projectiles, Swarm fires cluster bombs, and both are sure to turn the tide of battle in a flash. 

Also arriving with the Overdrive Update are two new abilities—Ghost Turret and Counterattack. Deploy turrets and reflect incoming damage to cause a splash. 

Saving the best for last, another Titan is entering the fray. Welcome the mother of all support Titans—Matriarch!

Get the lowdown on the Overdrive Update in the latest Dev Diary from Zukata, the Creative Director of War Robots: Frontiers.
