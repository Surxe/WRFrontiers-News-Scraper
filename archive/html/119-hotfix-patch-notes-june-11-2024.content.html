Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers and available following a short period of maintenance on June 11, 2024. 

BUG FIXES

  • [Weapon] Fixed a crash that could be caused by the Vortex.
  • [Pilot Talent] Fixed a crash that could be caused by Kate Sinclair's Wired To Win Talent.
  • Squad invitations are now automatically canceled after one minute.
  • Fixed a bug that could cause “retrieving of information” to display for player profiles.
