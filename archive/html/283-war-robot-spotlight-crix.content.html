Combat Analysis

  • War Robot Model: Crix
  • Class: Flanker
  • Dominion: FortEvo

When the storm takes hold, escape is no longer an option.

Crix excels in tactical disruption and sustained pursuit. It’s not the heaviest robot on the field, nor the fastest, but its control-oriented arsenal ensures that once it has you in its sights, retreat becomes impossible. 

By combining electromagnetic suppression with targeted energy depletion, Crix thrives in battles of endurance and attrition — ideal for cutting off evasive enemies or breaking defensive lines.

Under the Hood

Make the most of your War Robot by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, and Supply Gear.

Don’t forget, you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite elements!

  • Grappling (Core Gear): Unleashes a swarm of nanobots that latch onto the target over 10s, dragging the robot toward it and dealing 23,000. Cooldown: 45s.
  • Stormpike (Light, Electromagnetic, Special): Light arc cannon. Fires an electrically charged projectile that expands mid-flight.
  • Fuel Burn (Supply Gear): Burns 100% fuel from the target and blocks its regeneration for 5s. Cooldown 20s, 4 charges.
