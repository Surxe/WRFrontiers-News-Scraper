Enter the world of War Robots: Frontiers and play for free from July 10-18! All players can experience the full Early Access version of the game via MGLauncher or the Steam Demo Version for one week only, even if they don’t own a Pioneer Pack.

If you’re already a Pioneer, why not invite your friends to squad up and conquer the Wild Ten together with you? Don’t forget you can also share your customized Robot builds to put together an unstoppable team!

Since Early Access began, a whole host of new Robots, Pilots, Titans, weapons, and abilities have been added to the game, along with tons of new features. Check out what’s the in the latest update—Meet the Corpos—before jumping in. This is an excellent opportunity to test it all out and get a taste of all the content yet to come!

If you decide you want to take up residence in the Wild Ten, grab a Pioneer Pack which grants full Early Access to War Robots: Frontiers via wrfrontiers.com and Steam.

IMPORTANT: Any progress made during the Free Week will be carried over if you purchase a Pioneer Pack to continue your Early Access experience.
