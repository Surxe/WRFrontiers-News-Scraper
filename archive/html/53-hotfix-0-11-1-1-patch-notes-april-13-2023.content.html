These are the patch notes for the War Robots: Frontiers build available from April 13, 2023.

BUGS FIXED

  • Fixed Alpha (Titan) being unavailable to players who reached Level 10 prior to Update 0.11.1 (Overdrive).
  • Fixed battle results window not showing after the end of the match.
  • Fixed ability charge of chargeable abilities accumulating too fast when using Sticky Gun.
  • Fixed Napalm damage figures not popping up next to the damaged target.
  • Fixed the number of missile slots on the Vortex model not corresponding to the amount of ammo the weapon has.
  • Fixed certain issues with the secondary stats of the Passive Shield ability.
