Attention, Pilots! The following patch will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on November 11, 2025.

New Content

New War Robot: Crix

Command the storm with Crix, a relentless pursuer who pulls you into the fray, draining enemies' power and leaving them no chance of escape. Available in Daily Deals and Direct Offers.

New Weapon: Stormpike

Lightweight arc cannon optimized for quick strikes. Launches an energized projectile which expands in mid‑flight to increase hit area. Available in Daily Deals and Direct Offers.

New Hero Pilot: Giancarlo

Giancarlo's Rank 5: Ace Talent – Free Acceleration. Giancarlo can activate Dash Thrusters or Pneumatic Vault without consuming fuel. Talent has a cooldown 15s.

New Events

Intel Discount Event (November 11 – November 18)

This week, the Tortuga engineers invite Pilots to take a closer look at the following Builds:

  • War Robot Modules: Tyr, Loki, Phantom
  • Weapons: Lighter, Halo, Vortex
  • Gear: Energy Wall, Minefield, Homing Missile, Fuel Burn, Recon Mode, Ghost Turret

Gameplay Adjustments

Balance Changes

The following changes apply to the modules at max level:

Cyclops Shoulders

  • Increased Shield Regeneration from 3,150 to 3,400
  • Increased Shield Cooldown Reduction from 31% to 32%

Decker Shoulders

  • Decreased Armor from 84,500 to 81,000
  • Decreased Shield from 23,500 to 20,900
  • Decreased Shield Regeneration from 2,600 to 2,200
  • Decreased Shield Cooldown Reduction from 28% to 26%

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a typo in the Ceres description regarding cooldown time.
  • Potential crash fix for the Minefield gear.
  • Ricochet now hits the module it bounced off only once, in addition to all other modules.
  • Fixed an issue where the Chemical Hazard talent was not increasing the duration of damage-over-time (DoT) effects.
  • Fixed an issue where the Valkyrie decal was not properly centered on the robot.
  • Fixed an issue where the A/X (Select) hotkey icon was cropped in the Swap Robot menu when using a gamepad.
