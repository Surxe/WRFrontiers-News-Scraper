These are the patch notes for the War Robots: Frontiers build available from March 9, 2023. Update your game to the latest version and start playing in Early Access!

BALANCE CHANGES

  • Reduced the detonation delay of the Sharp Feathers ability projectiles on Raven.

BUGS FIXED

  • Fixed an issue that prevented players from auto-spawning their Titans following the destruction of their remaining Robots.
  • Fixed an issue that prevented Fowler weapons from dealing damage at large distances.
