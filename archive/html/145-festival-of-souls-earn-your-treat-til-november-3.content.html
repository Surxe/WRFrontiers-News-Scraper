The Day of the Dead is upon us, and War Robots: Frontiers is joining in with the festivities. Pilots can now participate in the Festival of Souls Event, active now until November 3 (23:59 CET). 

All Pilots playing via MY.GAMES Launcher and Steam can participate in this event, where winning three Battles will net you a terrific treat: the Sepulchre Sticker. 

This reward is permanent and will not be subject to future progress wipes.
