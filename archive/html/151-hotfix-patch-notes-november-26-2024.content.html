Attention, Pilots! The following hotfix will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on November 26, 2024. 

General Fixes & Improvements

  • The item rarity of the following items has been changed:
    • Tree Frog Paint Job → Epic
    • Freecon Security Paint Job → Epic
    • Desert Brawler Paint Job → Epic
  • New icons to showcase Weathering have been added to Robot customization.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug where Matriarch’s Ability “Drone Guard” would disappear after launching them up in the air. Drone Guard will now remain until they touch down to the ground.
  • Fixed a bug where Grim’s Ability “Grim Ripper” would deal damage beyond its intended range.
  • Fixed a bug where Alpha’s Shoulder Modules received a bigger increase in Shield Value when upgrading them from Level 12 to 13. Level 13 Modules now receive the correct shield value of 13.951.
  • Fixed a bug where the Pilot talent “Take a Punch” would light up Robot Modules in a bright light.
  • Fixed a bug where the Skybox (ceiling) was too low on some maps, causing Abilities like Alpha’s laser to hit the Skybox instead of the target in the area of effect.
