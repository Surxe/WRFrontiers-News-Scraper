These patch notes detail changes for the War Robots: Frontiers build available after maintenance on December 6, 2023.

BUGS FIXED

  • Fixed the Titan hangar slot incorrectly displaying as empty.
  • Fixed crashes when closing the News window.
  • Fixed robots moving forward from the spawn area without any input from the player.
  • Fixed Auto Spawn disabling in Team Deathmatch when the player loses all their robots.
  • Fixed blueprint modules adding progress in torso- and weapon-specific Contracts.
  • Fixed the amount of robots used by AI opponents being based on the lowest player level in a Squad rather than the highest.
  • Beacons in Tutorial mode can now be correctly captured.
