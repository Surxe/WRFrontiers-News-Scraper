From March 7 (09:00 CET) to March 21 (09:00 CET), you can win a War Robot for getting into a match with a Content Creator! All players will also receive 5,000 Credits for playing during this time.

To increase your chances of winning, join the matchmaking queue at the same time as a Content Creator that’s currently streaming for a chance to get into the same match. Of course, it helps if you’re watching their stream so you can join when they do! 

Check out the rest of the Spot A Streamer event information below, and good luck, Pilot! 

How To Participate & Rules

  • Participate between March 7 and March 21.
  • Complete the Tutorial and join matches, and if you match up with the corresponding Content Creator, you can choose one of the War Robots in the Prizes section as a reward!
  • Qualified matched participants will receive a survey via in-game news, be sure to check it and complete the questionnaire.
  • Participants can only receive one War Robot for the whole activity period in total. 
  • Players can receive multiple participant rewards (one for each event period).

Prizes

  • Regardless of whether they encounter Content Creators, all players who play during the time slots will receive 5,000 Credits.
  • Players who match with designated Content Creators during the specific timeslots will receive a survey to select one of the following War Robots: Ares, Griffin, Fury, Orochi.

Streamers:

 

Keep up to date with the schedule for Spot A Streamer on Discord to catch your favorite streamers in-game! Be sure to check individual streamer's social media channels for updates, too.

FAQ

If the Content Creator is streaming outside of the times mentioned and I get into a match with them, do I still get a reward? 

 

No, but check to see the times they are active during this event. If Content Creators overrun their assigned time, you will still be eligible to receive rewards in this case. 

How do I enter a match with a Content Creator?

 

Simply join the matchmaking queue for a chance to get into the same match as a Content Creator. Make sure you’re playing on the same server! 

What types of matches are considered valid for Spot a Streamer?

 

To receive the Participant Reward:

You will qualify if you play a match during the designated timeframe.

To receive the War Robot Reward: 

You will qualify for the event if you match with a Content Creator provided in the listing and participate in a match during the designated timeframe. You will still be qualified for this event regardless of whether you are on the allied or enemy team of the Content Creator as long as you matched with them during the event timeframe.

How do I know if I matched with the Content Creator successfully within the designated time frame?

 

The list of participating Content Creators can be found in the list above. As long as you get into a match with them during the designated event time frame, you will be considered eligible to receive a prize.

You can also watch the livestream of eligible Content Creators to check if you successfully matched with them during the timeframe.

Do I need to be on the same team as the Content Creator?

 

No. As long as you are in the same match, regardless of what team you’re on, you are eligible to receive the prize.

Can I match the Content Creators across different servers?

 

No, you must be on the same server to be able to match against them.

Do I have to play the whole match?

 

Yes. We would like to encourage players to play matches fairly and participate actively in the battle by aiming for the match objective with their team. As part of match verification, the team will be reviewing match activity logs and leaver logs to determine if the match was paired fairly and as intended.

Do I have to win the match?

 

No! Winning a match is NOT a requirement to receive any of the rewards.

How can I know if I am qualified to receive a Participation Reward?

 

You play during the designated event timeframe. Make sure to check your time zone! 

How can I know if I am qualified to receive a War Robot?

 

You will receive an in-game news pop-up once the event has concluded on March 24th at 10:00 CET. Please remember to check your Notifications if you missed it. If you receive this pop-up, we highly encourage you to complete the questionnaire promptly and select your War Robot reward.

Players who do not fill the survey will not receive their reward. The War Robot rewards will be sent at the end of the event on April 3, 10:00 CET.

If I match with multiple Content Creators, can I receive multiple War Robots?

 

No. Each player can only receive one War Robot from the entirety of the event.

I play on Console, can I join this event?

 

Yes! Players who play War Robots: Frontiers on Xbox or PlayStation can participate as long as you have enabled cross-platform play to engage in a quick match.

Console players, please check the following steps to make sure you turn this function on:

  • PlayStation: Cross-play is enabled by default. 
  • Xbox: Enable cross-play by following: How do multiplayer games and communication work for cross-network play? | Xbox Support.

What if I have a question for Spot a Streamer that isn't shared here?

 

If you encounter a problem that is not addressed in this FAQ, please feel free to visit our official Discord or contact our Customer Support team.
