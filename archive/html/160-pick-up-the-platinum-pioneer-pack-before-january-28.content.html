Changes to Pioneer Packs

As we approach the launch of War Robots: Frontiers in 2025, we’re making a few important changes to Pioneer Packs:

  • Varangian Sticker: This cosmetic for your War Robots is now available in the Iron, Steel, Titanium, and Platinum Pioneer Packs (and will be retroactively added to your Inventory if you have already purchased a Pioneer Pack). Thanks for playing with us in Early Access—we hope this exclusive bonus reminds you of all the fun you’ve had, how far the game has come, and inspires you for the battles that lie ahead!
  • Kate Sinclair (Hero Pilot): Kate Sinclair (Hero Pilot) will be available for purchase in the Store in the future. Hero Pilots are an important part of the world of War Robots: Frontiers, and we want to make sure players new and old can experience it to the fullest with their favorite Pilots at the helm.
  • Pricing Changes: As we’ve rebalanced the game economy, the value of items featured in Pioneer Packs has increased. This will not increase the price of Pioneer Packs, but affects the discount values you see on the website and Steam.
  • Availability: The Platinum Pioneer Pack will be removed from sale on January 28. Other Pioneer Packs will be removed from sale close to the release of War Robots: Frontiers. Stay tuned for more information about this.
