Attention, Pilots! The following update for War Robots: Frontiers will be deployed after a short maintenance period on February 10, 2026.

New Content

New Battle Pass: Orbital Nexus (February 10 – March 23)

Unlock the Norna Titan, Slugger and Zenit weapons, and valuable resources. Collect Wraps and Stickers as you progress and prove your skills on the battlefield.

New Titan Weapons: Skuld & Conflux

  • Skuld (Norna) — Assault plasma autocannon. Deadly at close to medium range, but barely scratches armor at longer distances. Available in Daily Deals and Direct Offers.
  • Conflux (Volta) — Heavy energy autocannon that fires twin energy projectiles with extended range. Available in Daily Deals and Direct Offers.

New Events

Intel Discount Event (February 10–17)

This week, the Tortuga engineers invite Pilots to take a closer look at the following builds:

  • War Robot Modules: Loki, Tyr, Scorpion, Norna (Titan)
  • Weapons: Halo, Vortex, Tusk, Coriolis (Norna Titan)
  • Gear: Fuel Burn, Recon Mode, Ghost Turret, Homing Missile, Repulsor, Gamma Beam

Solidus Node: Umbra Is Back (February 10–24)

Dive back into the industrial shadows of Umbra, available in a dedicated unranked queue. Expect tight corridors, layered cover, and brutal close-quarters firefights.

Gameplay Adjustments

Leagues

League placements will be reset, and ranks adjusted for Season 4. League Points will be assigned based on your new League placement:

  • Bronze: 0 League Points (bottom of Bronze League)
  • Silver: 300 League Points (bottom of Silver League)
  • Gold & Platinum: 500 League Points (bottom of Gold League)
  • Emerald, Diamond & Noachium: 1500 League Points (bottom of Emerald League)

Ranked Matchmaking

Ranked matchmaking brackets have been restructured:

  • Bronze players can now be matched with Silver players
  • Gold players can now be matched with Platinum players
  • Emerald, Diamond, and Noachium players can now be matched together

The maximum queue time for crews of four or more players has been reduced from 5 minutes to 2.5 minutes.

Player Progression

Player account progression has been expanded. The maximum player level has been increased to 230.

New crafting unlocks:

  • Level 203: Decker Shoulder Modules
  • Level 206: Decker Chassis Modules
  • Level 209: Supply Surge Cycle Gear
  • Level 212: Hefty Weapon
  • Level 215: Decker Torso Modules
  • Level 218: Crix Shoulder Modules
  • Level 221: Crix Chassis Modules
  • Level 227: Stormpike Weapon
  • Level 230: Crix Torso Modules
