Updated Last Robot Standing Mode (August 26–September 2)

Power up, Pilots! The Last Robot Standing mode just got an upgrade! From now until September 2, a new temporary ruleset introduces strategically placed power-ups across the map—giving your entire team a boost!

  • Shield: Provides shield for the whole team that picks it up.
  • Mass Regeneration: Regenerates armor of each Module for the whole team that picks it up.
  • Mass Armor: Increases damage resistance by 20% for the whole team that picks it up.
  • Mass Fuel: Increases restoration speed of fuel by 20% for the whole team that picks it up.
  • Mass Speed: Increases acceleration and speed by 20% for the whole team that picks it up.
  • Mass Ultimate: Adds a charge to Cycle Gear for the whole team that picks it up.

Intel Discount Event (August 26–September 9)

From August 26 (11:00 CEST/02:00 PT) to September 9 (11:00 CEST/02:00 PT), the Intel cost for upgrading Raven, Loki, and Phantom Modules, Weapons, and Supply and Cycle Gear will be reduced.
