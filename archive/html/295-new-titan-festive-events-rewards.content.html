New Common Pilot: Rex “Rope” Cole

Something on its chestplate looks familiar, something legendary...

Every war has its entertainers. This winter, Rex Cole takes the stage.

Dressed in Christmas-themed gear and armed with an explosive sense of showmanship, Rex doesn’t just fight battles, he performs them. Where others see cover and caution, Rex sees timing, momentum, and the perfect moment to light up the battlefield.

Pilot Talents

  • Feed Hacker – Your weapons reload 5% faster.
  • Gun Nut – Your shotguns and special weapons deal 4% more damage.
  • Gear Resonator – Using any gear reduces incoming damage by 7% for 5 seconds.
  • Closing the Deal – If your team has more kills than the enemy, your weapons reload 6% faster.
  • Afterparty – Each kill triggers a brief celebratory fireworks effect.

Bold, festive, and unapologetically loud, Rex Cole is ready to bring holiday flair and explosive performance to the front lines.

Note: Common Pilot Rex “Rope” Cole is available only during the holiday period, but may appear in the Store at a later date.
