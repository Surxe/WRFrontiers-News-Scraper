As a Wild Ten Pilot, it’s good to get to grips with your partner in battle—the War Robot. With several War Robots to choose from, each with its own unique attributes, picking the best ally for each fight is crucial.

Unleash chaos with Decker, a spider-like Assault built for mid-range havoc and rapid chain attacks.

Combat Analysis

  • War Robot Model: Decker
  • Class: Assault
  • Dominion: FortEvo

Decker thrives in tight quarters, using its agility to get up close and personal. Its signature Core Gear, Chain Overload, hacks enemy robots with a virus that deals massive damage over time—and can jump between nearby foes, turning clustered enemies into a ticking time bomb.

Equipped with Hefty, a dual-barrel heavy autocannon, Decker can sustain medium-range fire when needed, but it truly shines in chaotic, close-range brawls.

Under the Hood

Make the most of your War Robot by learning about its Core Gear and recommended Weapons, Supply Gear, and Cycle Gear.

Don’t forget, you can always customize any War Robot with your favorite elements!

  • Chain Overload (Core Gear): Hacks an enemy robot with a virus that deals 22,800 damage per second for 6 seconds. The virus jumps to other enemies within 75m, chaining up to 8 times.
  • Hefty (Kinetic, Autocannon, Heavy): Dual-barrel heavy autocannon for sustained medium-range fire.
  • Supply Surge (Cycle Gear): After 0.2 seconds of preparation, restores 1 charge and reduces Supply Gear cooldown by 100%.
  • Emergency Shield (Supply Gear): Restores 6,000 shield per second for 6 seconds.
