As of the latest update, Decker and Crix, the highly anticipated War Robot Spiders, are now accessible through the crafting progression system. Players who have reached the necessary levels can craft these powerful robots to take their combat strategies to the next level.

Decker

  • Level 215 - Decker Torso

Crix

  • Level 218 - Crix Shoulder
  • Level 221 - Crix Chassis
  • Level 227 - Stormpike Weapon
  • Level 230 - Crix Torso
