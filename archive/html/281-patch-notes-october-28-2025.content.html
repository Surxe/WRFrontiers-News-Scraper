Attention, Pilots! The following patch will be deployed for War Robots: Frontiers following a short period of maintenance on October 28, 2025.

New Content

New War Robot: Decker

Meet Decker, the spider-like Assault War Robot, equipped with the powerful Core Gear, Chain Overload. Pair it with the new Hefty Weapon, a dual-barrel heavy autocannon designed for relentless medium-range fire. Available in Daily Deals and Direct Offers.

New Weapon: Hefty

Dual-barrel heavy autocannon for sustained medium-range fire. Available in Daily Deals and Direct Offers.

New Gear: Supply Surge

After 1.2 seconds of preparation, restores 1 charge and reduces Supply Gear cooldown by 60%. Available in Daily Deals and Direct Offers.

New Map: Tortuga

A new map, Tortuga, is now live! Explore the blend of cityscapes and lush holographic parks, perfect for mid-range duels and close-quarters brawls.

New Hero Pilots

New hero pilots, Emma James and "Dredge" Collins, have been added for Halloween.

Emma James

  • Ace Talent: Ghost Visor. Enemies that hit Emma's mech become marked: visible, focusable, and take 4% more damage from weapons for 6 seconds. Talent has a cooldown of 16 seconds.

"Dredge" Collins

  • Ace Talent: Fragments Synergy. Every assist you make increases the primary and secondary parameters of your signature ability by 10% for 15 seconds. The effect stacks up to 4 times.

New Events

Intel Discount Event (October 28 – November 4)

This week, the Tortuga engineers invite Pilots to take a closer look at the following Builds:

  • War Robot Modules: Pursuer, Fury, Varangian
  • Weapons: Pulsar, Orkan, Scatter
  • Gear: Energy Wall, Recon Mode, Fuel Burn, Flash Bang, Blink, Homing Missile

Painted and Dangerous! (October 28 – November 4)

  • Log in daily and play 6 battles each day to claim your spooky Halloween rewards.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a bug where the VFX for Alpha’s Magnetic Sensor would disappear while the ability was still active.
  • Fixed a bug causing the aim to be incorrect when using Kinetic Pulse.
  • Fixed a bug that caused the camera to blur when inspecting the Anansi and Kumo modules in the Rewards and Store sections.
  • Fixed a bug in Alpha’s Firestorm ability where enemies could remain highlighted after Alpha’s death.
  • Fixed a bug where invisibility would remain active on the War Robot if players held the fire button before activating the ability.
  • Fixed a bug where the Homing Salvo did not increase in damage when leveled up.
