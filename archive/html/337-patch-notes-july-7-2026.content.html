Attention, Pilots! The following update for War Robots: Frontiers will be deployed after a short maintenance period on July 7, 2026.

New Content

  • New Game mode: Quantum Core
  • New Battle Pass: Faded Glory
  • New War Robot: Relic Lancelot
  • New Weapon: Relic Thunder
  • Balance Changes
  • Bugfixes

New Game mode: Quantum Core

Capture the enemy Core and bring it to your base, but don't forget to defend your own as you can only score when both Cores are merged. Team up, secure a 3-point lead, and claim victory. Merge the Cores, Pilots!

New Battle Pass: Faded Glory (July 7 – August 3)

Unlock two robots at once: the latest Relic Lancelot and Artemis as well as Velite weapons and much more!

New War Robot: Relic Lancelot

Reborn of Mayflower's classic Defender. Trading a light weapon slot for reinforced shoulder armor plates, this Relic variant elevates survivability to unprecedented heights. To offset the reduced firepower, its modified torso now accommodates a heavy weapon slot, while the Paladin's Ward ability transforms Lancelot into a true frontline juggernaut capable of absorbing massive damage and repairing nearby allies.

Starting July 7, Relic Lancelot will be available through Daily Deals, direct offers and the new Battle pass.

New Robot Weapon: Relic Thunder

Iconic shotgun goes Relic. While its increased weight demands heavier configurations, this Relic variant rewards pilots with lower energy consumption and more firepower.

Starting July 7, Relic Thunder will be available through Daily Deals and direct offers.

Balance Changes

Velite

  • Temp of Fire decreased from 89 rpm to 68 rpm

Horde

  • Armor Damage decreased from 21000 to 17000

Railgun

  • Armor Damage increased from 4900 to 6400
  • Shield Damage decreased from 5750 to 5500

Harpy Shoulder

  • Shield Amount increased from 26620 to 28800

Pursuer Chassis

  • Armor increased from 53200 to 57680
  • Fuel increased from 19 to 20

Heike Chassis

  • Fuel decreased from 20 to 18

Typhon

  • Blackout: Jam reload slowdown increased from 1.5 to 2
  • Blackout: Jam duration increased from 8 to 10

Bugfixes

  • Fixed a bug where the Relic Bulgasari chassis would revert to the default Bulgasari visuals when using abilities.
  • Fixed a bug where the Wyrm's ability visual effect was occasionally invisible during activation.
