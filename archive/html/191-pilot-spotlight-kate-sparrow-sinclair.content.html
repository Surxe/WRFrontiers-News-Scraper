Pilots are the final puzzle piece in your loadout, and choosing the ideal companion for your War Robot is critical to your success…or failure. 

Meet Kate “Sparrow” Sinclair, a grease monkey with a point to prove on the Wild Ten. 

BACKGROUND ANALYSIS

  • Name: Kate Sinclair
  • Call Sign: Sparrow
  • Class: Assault
  • Dominion: Mayflower

Kate Sinclair led an idyllic life, but it was one without adventure. Through this boredom, she discovered modding, and it became a lifelong passion. Studying alone and breaking into the piloting world, quashing local rebellions soon became dull, and she longed for the real thing—warfare.

Kate is slowly but surely discovering that her dream of life on the Wild Ten is far from reality. This bright, talented mechanic is rarely found without a wrench in her hand, but her curiosity is often mistaken for naivety. While she isn’t street-smart, this rookie has a lot to prove and isn’t afraid to make mistakes along the way. Her blind enthusiasm is both her greatest weakness and her greatest strength.

TACTICAL TALENTS

Every Pilot has Talents, but it’s up to you to pick the best ones for battle. Pick one Talent per Rank, and put Kate through her paces.

Rank 1: General Talents

  • Gearhead: Gear goes off cooldown faster.
  • Case-Hardened: You take less damage from all sources.
  • Feed Hacker: Your weapons reload quicker.

Rank 2: Assault Talents (Class Specific)

  • Gun Nut: Your autocannons and rocket launchers deal more damage.
  • Maximizer: For every damage you deal, instantly regenerates some shield.
  • Offensive Tactics: Boosts primary and secondary stats of Attack gear.

Rank 3: Smart Talents (Personality Specific)

  • Backup Plan: Whenever all your gear is on cooldown, activates a shield.
  • Air Warrior: If you are in the air, deal more damage against armor.
  • Synergist: Assists reduce cooldown of your gear.

Rank 4: Mayflower Talents (Dominion Specific)

  • Red Alert: Taking damage speeds up your weapon reload.
  • Survival Instinct: If any Module has low armor, you take less damage from all sources.
  • Quick Reflexes: Activating any defensive gear reduces shield cooldown.

Rank 5: Ace Talent 

Kate acquired this Talent through hard work and dedication.

  • Deflector Gizmo: After losing shields, reflects damage back at the enemy.
