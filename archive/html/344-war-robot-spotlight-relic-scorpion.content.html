As a Wild Ten Pilot, it’s good to get to grips with your partner in battle—the War Robot. With several War Robots to choose from, each with its own unique attributes, picking the best ally for each fight is crucial.

Built for smart plays and map control, Relic Scorpion takes the ambush playstyle to the next level. Trading raw armor for long-range mobility, this Relic variant dominates by striking from unexpected angles, catching backline targets off guard, and capturing key objectives before enemies can react.

Starting August 25, Relic Scorpion will be available through Daily Deals, direct offers and the new Battle pass.

Combat Analysis

  • War Robot Model: Relic Scorpion
  • Class: Flanker
  • Dominion: FortEvo

Relic Scorpion is a fast, strategic assassin, perfect for Pilots who prefer map awareness and timing over brutal head-on fights. Relic model rewards a careful approach—ideal for hunting down long-range artillery on remote perches or hunting wounded enemies retreating for repair.

While Relic Scorpion lacks the armor to survive inside heavy frontline crossfire, it shines at disrupting enemy lines. Teleportation ability covers huge distances, allowing you to instantly close in on isolated targets, escape dangerous ambushes, or snag open beacons across the map. Just keep in mind that its light frame won't survive a mistake; jumping straight into a group of enemies is a fast ticket back to the hangar.

For maximum impact, pair the robot with the new Relic Pinata. Re-engineered as a primary weapon, this close-range rocket launcher shares the precise firing profile of the Relic Orkan. It offers consistent DPS and tight, controllable rocket bursts—trading the automatic fire of standard rocket pods for refined fire management and lethal point-blank firepower.
