These patch notes detail changes for the War Robots: Frontiers build available from September 12, 2023.

TECHNICAL CHANGES

  • Removed the SMAA option from antialiasing settings.
  • Fixed changes not applying to the Resolution Scale setting when TSR upscaling is selected.
  • Increased Elimination Mode match duration to 18 minutes.

BALANCE CHANGES

  • Matriarch Shotguns: Reduced accuracy, reduced maximum range to 350 meters, reduced the number of particles per shot to 10, and reduced damage to the core by 25%.
  • Grim Cannon: Increased damage to the core by 20%.
  • Scatter: Reduced damage to armor by 10% and reduced damage to the core by 25%.
  • AI bot difficulty decreased for players up to and including Level 25.
