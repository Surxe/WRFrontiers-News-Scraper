Exciting news for content creators! We’re thrilled to announce that revenue sharing is now available for members of the MY.GAMES Content Creator Program.

If you’d like to participate in revenue sharing, take a look at our Revenue Sharing Creator Guide to find instructions on how to sign up. 

The MY.GAMES Content Creator Program already has some great benefits including:

  • Earning rewards & resources
  • Participating in exclusive events
  • Access to the teams behind the games

These benefits are even better now we've partnered with Affise to bring this new opportunity to creators! With revenue sharing, you’ll be able to earn more from your content and collaborations with MY.GAMES.

To kick things off, revenue sharing will first be available for War Robots: Frontiers, where creators can earn 25% of all purchases made on MY.GAMES Market for the first two months when players enter your Creator code.

If you're not yet a member of the MY.GAMES Content Creator Program, now is the perfect time to join! Enroll today and start benefiting from revenue sharing, exclusive rewards, and more.

Revenue Sharing is currently only available for War Robots: Frontiers on PC (Steam and MY.GAMES Launcher) but we have plans to expand to many more platforms and MY.GAMES titles in the future. This is just the beginning, and we’re working hard to bring this opportunity to even more games in our portfolio.

Stay tuned for updates as we continue to roll out new ways to support and reward our amazing creator community!
