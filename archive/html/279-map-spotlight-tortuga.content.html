Each battlefield in War Robots: Frontiers has its own legends. Tortuga, the mercenaries’ home and heart, is about to write the next one.

Field Reconnaissance

Built by Freecon more than a decade ago, Tortuga began as a space station for workers and engineers maintaining the first Warp Gates. When a revolt turned the site into a free mercenary hub, it became the beating heart of the Frontier — a home to Pilots, traders, and outlaws alike.

Now this neutral ground is no longer safe. The Dominions’ struggle for power has reached Tortuga’s core. What was once your Hangar — your sanctuary — is now a warzone. The fight for control of Dominion Plaza will decide who commands the station… and who shapes the future of the Frontier.

Strategic Analysis

Tortuga offers a blend of artificial cityscapes and natural terrain — a balance of open plazas, narrow lanes, and lush holographic parks that conceal more than they reveal.

Whether you prefer mid-range duels or close-quarters brawls, Tortuga rewards precision, coordination, and adaptability. Titans must watch their height in the confined corridors, while lighter War Robots can exploit vertical layers and alternate routes for sudden flanks.
