NEW ROBOTS: OROCHI AND PURIFIER

Pilot two brand-new Robots in the Spring Update, each with their own unique Ability.

  • Orochi:  Orochi highlights enemies, pings them, and prepares for a team fight with its allies using its Spotlight ability.
  • Purifier: Purifier keeps its allies close, buffing them as they head into battle with its Zealot ability.

NEW MAP: CATALYST

Watch your step—the new volcanic map has more to worry about than just your enemies! Battle on the new Catalyst Map, where lava can take you out just as fast as a foe. 

NEW WEAPONS & ABILITIES

  • New Weapon - Rampant: Cause major AoE damage with this light grenade launcher. 
  • New Weapon - Incinerator: Burn up the competition with this new flamethrower.
  • New Ability - Minefield: Slow enemies and inflict damage with this new Ability.

NEW FEATURES

  • Firing Range: Test out your Robot and Titan builds on the new Firing Range!
  • Titan Constructor: Upgrade and customize your fearsome friends in the new Titan Constructor.
