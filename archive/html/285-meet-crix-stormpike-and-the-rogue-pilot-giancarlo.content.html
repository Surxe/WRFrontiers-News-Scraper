New War Robot: Crix

When the storm takes hold, escape is no longer an option.

Crix excels in tactical disruption and sustained pursuit. It’s not the heaviest robot on the field, nor the fastest, but its control-oriented arsenal ensures that once it has you in its sights, retreat becomes impossible. 

By combining electromagnetic suppression with targeted energy depletion, Crix thrives in battles of endurance and attrition — ideal for cutting off evasive enemies or breaking defensive lines.

Crix is now available in the Store and Daily Deals.

New Hero Pilot: Giancarlo

  • Name: Giancarlo
  • Class: Flanker
  • Dominion: Mayflower

Unleash Giancarlo Cruz (available in the Store) as your War Robot’s new Pilot. A former Mayflower soldier turned traitor after losing both eyes in battle, he turned to the Morgan Crime Family for illegal augmentations. Now, branded a criminal and hunted on all sides, Giancarlo’s mechanical enhancements give him deadly precision and faster reloads.

Tactical Talents

Every Pilot has Talents, but it’s up to you to pick the best ones for battle. Pick one Talent per Rank.

Rank 1: General Talents

  • Gearhead: Gear goes off cooldown faster.
  • Case-Hardened: You take less damage from all sources.
  • Feed Hacker: Your weapons reload quicker.

Rank 2: Flanker Talents (Class Specific)

  • Gun Nut: Your shotguns and special weapons deal more damage.
  • Shields Expert: Boosts shield.
  • Fast Reflexes: Boosts primary and secondary stats of Movement Gear.

Rank 3: Sneaky Talents (Personality Specific)

  • Fuel Thief: Hitting an enemy that has not damaged you recently restores fuel.
  • Gear Resonator: Using any Gear reduces incoming damage by a small amount for a brief period.
  • Parasite Protocol: Every elimination increases a random parameter of your Core Gear.

Rank 4: Mayflower Talents (Dominion Specific)

  • Red Alert: Taking damage speeds up your weapon reload
  • Survival Instinct: If any Module has less than 30% armor, you take less damage from all sources.
  • Quick Reflexes: Activating any defensive Gear reduces shield cooldown.

Rank 5: Ace Talent 

  • Free Acceleration: Giancarlo can activate Dash Thrusters or Pneumatic Vault without consuming fuel. Talent has a cooldown 15s.
