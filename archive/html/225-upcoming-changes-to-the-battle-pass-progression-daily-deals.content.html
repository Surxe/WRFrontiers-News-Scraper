Season 2: Power Surge is set to bring a cargo load of new improvements and changes to War Robots: Frontiers. Alongside a fresh delivery of seasonal content and rewards, we’ve also been working on important changes to the Battle Pass, account progression, and what’s available in Daily Deals to expand construction and customization possibilities for all players. 

Read on to learn more, including details about upcoming events in War Robots: Frontiers!

Battle Pass: Shipment Unlock Timer Removed

In Season 1: Blockade, new Shipments for the Battle Pass were added on a regular basis, unlocking access to new pages of rewards throughout the duration of the Battle Pass. Starting with Season 2: Power Surge and the Scavenger’s Haul Battle Pass on June 17, all Battle Pass rewards will be available to unlock from day one. Earn those Battle Tokens to be among the first Pilots to construct Loki!

Battle Pass Rewards: Intel Added to Free Progression

Intel can now be obtained from the Battle Pass for free. Starting with the Scavenger’s Haul Battle Pass (launching June 17), you’ll be able to earn up to 200 Intel through free progression, a crucial resource used to upgrade your best Builds.

Battle Pass Content Coming to Daily Deals on Day One

War Robots and Weapons will launch in Daily Deals at the same time they’re available in the Battle Pass! This change is designed to give Pilots more options for unlocking battle gear, without having to spend WarpReals.

Season 2: Power Surge will introduce Loki Modules and the Halo Weapon to Daily Deals at the beginning of the Season, with Scorpion Modules and the Tusk Weapon from Season 1: Blockade also joining the Daily Deals roster.

We’ve also rebalanced the Battle Pass and Free progression to give you even more Credits, Salvage, and Intel throughout the Pass. You can still complete at least one full Shipment (plus change) without upgrading your Pass, but Free progression will not award enough Battle Tokens to obtain the Torso or Chassis Modules on Shipment 2 and beyond. However, keep an eye on Daily Deals, and you can spend your Credits on the Modules and Weapons you seek!

Daily Deals Refresh Changes

If you’re hunting for that perfect Module or Weapon, your first Daily Refresh will now cost 700 Credits instead of WarpReals. This is designed to increase your chances of getting the Daily Deal you want, without spending WarpReals. You should be able to earn this amount of Credits from a single Daily Job, so happy hunting and shopping, Pilots!

Further Daily Refreshes beyond your first will still cost WarpReals, with more Refreshes available at rising increments (starting at 15 WarpReals for your first, and increasing by 10 each time up to a maximum of 45 WarpReals). 

Daily Deals Chances

You can check a new chance table on Daily Deals to view your percentage chance of seeing certain items based on their rarity. This is designed to increase transparency on the rewards available and set expectations on what you might find there.

Account Progression: New Account Levels & Rewards

More Account Levels and rewards have been added! Players can now progress to Level 95 (up from Level 80). The new progression rewards include a Cyclops War Robot (Factory Build), with new Modules available at every third Level (Levels 83, 86, 89, 92, 95). 30 Intel will be awarded at every other Level (e.g. Levels 81, 82, 84, etc.)

Even more Account Levels and progression rewards (including Scorpion Modules) will be added alongside the Spark of Ingenuity Battle Pass, launching July 29.

Season 2: Upcoming Events

Plug into Season 2: Power Surge when it launches on June 17 to enjoy celebratory events:

  • Twitch Drops: Tune in to War Robots: Frontiers Content Creators on Twitch to earn rewards!
  • Intel Catch-up: A special event for returning and new players to help get your Builds up to scratch.
  • Pilot XP Event: Put your Pilots through a rigorous boot camp to bring out their best in battle.
  • New Credit Offers: Idy Demerost (Hero Pilot) and Fury (War Robot) with Pulsar Weapons will be available to buy for Credits, replacing Kate Sinclair (Hero Pilot) and Griffin (War Robot).
  • New Titan Weapon Coming to Daily Deals: Jigsaw, a new Titan Weapon built for Grim, will be available via Daily Deals for a limited time (stay tuned for more details).

Keep your eyes on your comms, Pilots!
