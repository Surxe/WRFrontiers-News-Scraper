Quantum Core is a brand-new 6v6 PvP mode in War Robots: Frontiers.

The focus of this mode is on two Cores. Capture the enemy Core and bring it to your Base to score, but don't forget to protect your own Core at the same time. You can't score a point unless both Cores are secured at your base. The first team to get a 3-point lead wins instantly. If time runs out, the team with the most points wins.

But remember, carrying the enemy Core slows your War Robot down by 20%.

Destroying a carrier robot causes them to drop the Core. If it is your team's Core, stand near it for 5 seconds to return it to your base. Enemies can pick it up again to keep moving. Note that you cannot retrieve a Core if an enemy is standing nearby, so clear the area first. Grab the Cores your allies lost to save the day!

Extra Warp Gates can be captured by the teams for more deployment options. However, you can't retrieve your Core or capture Warp Gates if you're holding the enemy Core. Stick to one goal at a time!

Robot redeployment follows the Team Deathmatch rules to keep the action going.

Merge the Cores and secure the win, Pilots!
