Join the Community Events on Discord

Looking to squad up, sharpen your strategies, or just have fun with fellow Pilots? Our community events are a great way for both newcomers and veterans to jump into teamplay, share builds, discuss tactics, and connect with the War Robots: Frontiers community on Discord.

Friday Play Sessions

Join us every Friday at 19:00 CEST for Community Play Sessions, hosted by DivinityDozer. With open lobbies and casual matches, it’s the perfect opportunity to team up, exchange strategies, test builds, and enjoy the battlefield together.

Saturday Night Fights

Hosted by ItzGladzilla and NukerTheGreat, Saturday Night Fights features weekly battles—from one-on-one duels to full 6v6 draft matches. It’s a place to test your skills, enjoy competitive teamplay, and see how far you can go.

Weekend Warfare

Weekend Warfare is our community-driven 6v6 scrim series, starting on May 8, open to both solo Pilots and full squads. Join the action for tactical team battles, coordinated squad play, and a chance to put your strategies to the test throughout the weekend. Be sure to pre-register on our Discord to participate!
